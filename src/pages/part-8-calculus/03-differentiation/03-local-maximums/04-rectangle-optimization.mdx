Here is the strategy in action on a concrete example.

<Callout kind="example" title="Rectangle with perimeter 20 — maximum area">
Label the sides $x$ and $10 - x$, so the area is
$$f(x) = x(10 - x) = 10x - x^2 \qquad f \colon [0, 10] \to \RR.$$

By <Term k="extremeValueThm">compactness</Term>, a global maximum exists. Any interior maximum satisfies
$$0 = f'(x) = 10 - 2x \implies x = 5.$$
Candidates: $x \in \{0, 5, 10\}$.

Evaluating: $f(0) = 0$, $f(5) = 25$, $f(10) = 0$.

The maximum area $25$ is achieved by the $5 \times 5$ square; the minimum area $0$ is achieved by the degenerate rectangles.
</Callout>
