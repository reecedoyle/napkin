Here is a direct application of the <Term k="meanValueThm">MVT</Term> that captures intuition about "faster means further ahead."

<Callout kind="proposition" title="Racetrack principle">
Let $f, g \colon \RR \to \RR$ be differentiable with $f(0) = g(0)$.

**(a)** If $f'(x) \ge g'(x)$ for every $x > 0$, then $f(x) \ge g(x)$ for every $x > 0$.

**(b)** If $f'(x) > g'(x)$ for every $x > 0$, then $f(x) > g(x)$ for every $x > 0$.
</Callout>

Think of it as two runners starting at the same position: if one is always at least as fast, they end up at least as far ahead.
