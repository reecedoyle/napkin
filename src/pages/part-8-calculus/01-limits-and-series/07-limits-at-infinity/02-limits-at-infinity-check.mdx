Arithmetic works for limits at infinity in the same way as for limits at a point:

$$\lim_{x\to\infty}(f(x) \pm g(x)) = \lim_{x\to\infty} f(x) \pm \lim_{x\to\infty} g(x),$$

and similarly for products and quotients (when the denominator limit is nonzero). This is essentially the same proof as for sequences, adapted to the "large $x$" setup.
