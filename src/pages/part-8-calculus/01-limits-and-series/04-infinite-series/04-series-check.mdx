The "partial sums" framework also answers a long-standing philosophical complaint: how can a sum of rationals be irrational?

The point is that $\sum_{n=1}^\infty \frac{1}{n^2}$ is the *limit* of the partial sums $\frac{1}{1}, \frac{1}{1}+\frac{1}{4}, \ldots$, and we know sequences of rationals can converge to irrationals. No mystery.
