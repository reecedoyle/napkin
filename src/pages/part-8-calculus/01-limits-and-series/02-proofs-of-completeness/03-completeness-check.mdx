Let's consolidate. We have two big facts about $\RR$:

1. **Sup/inf existence**: every nonempty bounded subset of $\RR$ has a sup and an inf.
2. **Completeness**: every <Term k="cauchySequence">Cauchy sequence</Term> in $\RR$ converges.

Fact 1 is built into the *definition* of $\RR$ via <Term k="dedekindCut">Dedekind cuts</Term>. Fact 2 then follows from fact 1, as we showed with the knife argument.

<Callout kind="question" title="A quick sanity check">
The set $\QQ \cap [0, 2]$ (rationals between 0 and 2) is bounded. Does it have a sup in $\QQ$? Does it have a sup in $\RR$?
</Callout>
