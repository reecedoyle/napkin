Since 1 + pⁿ ≡ 1 (mod p), the denominator is a p-adic unit, so νₚ(qₙ − 2021) = νₚ(2021) + n → ∞. Hence |qₙ − 2021|ₚ → 0, i.e., qₙ → 2021 in ℚₚ."
