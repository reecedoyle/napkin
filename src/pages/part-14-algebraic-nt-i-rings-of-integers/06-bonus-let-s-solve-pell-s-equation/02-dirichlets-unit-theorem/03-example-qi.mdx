Let us apply <Term k="an58dirichletUnitTheorem">Dirichlet's unit theorem</Term> to the Gaussian integers $\ZZ[i]$.

$K = \QQ(i)$ has <Term k="an58signature">signature</Term> $(r_1, r_2) = (0, 1)$: no real embeddings, one conjugate pair of complex embeddings. So:
$$s = r_1 + r_2 - 1 = 0 + 1 - 1 = 0.$$

With $s = 0$ there are **no free generators** — the only units are the roots of unity. Since $\NK(a + bi) = a^2 + b^2 = 1$ in the integers forces $a, b \in \{-1, 0, 1\}$, we get:
$$\mathcal{O}_K^\times = \ZZ[i]^\times = \{\pm 1, \pm i\}.$$
