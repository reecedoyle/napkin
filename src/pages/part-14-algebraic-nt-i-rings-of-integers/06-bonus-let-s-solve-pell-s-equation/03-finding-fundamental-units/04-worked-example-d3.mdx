Let us find the <Term k="an58fundamentalUnit">fundamental unit</Term> of $\ZZ[\sqrt{3}]$ by solving the <Term k="an58pellEquationPM">Pell-type equation</Term> $x^2 - 3y^2 = \pm 1$.

We search for the smallest positive $y$ and corresponding integer $x$:
- $y = 1$: $x^2 = 3 + \varepsilon$ where $\varepsilon = \pm 1$. So $x^2 \in \{2, 4\}$. We have $x = 2$! Solution: $(2, 1)$, giving unit $2 + \sqrt{3}$ with norm $4 - 3 = 1$.

That's it. The fundamental unit is $u = 2 + \sqrt{3}$, and:
$$\ZZ[\sqrt{3}]^\times = \{\pm (2+\sqrt{3})^n \mid n \in \ZZ\}.$$
