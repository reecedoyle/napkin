Since N(u) = +1 and the norm is multiplicative, every power uⁿ has N(uⁿ) = N(u)ⁿ = 1ⁿ = 1. The units of ℤ[√34] are {±uⁿ : n ∈ ℤ}, and each has norm +1 (since N(−α) = N(α) for the norm in a real quadratic field). Therefore no element of ℤ[√34] has norm −1, which means x² − 34y² = −1 has no integer solutions. The condition '−1 is a quadratic residue mod 34' is necessary but not sufficient: it ensures there is no local obstruction mod 34, but there can still be a global obstruction coming from the sign of the fundamental unit's norm."
