<SlideLayout title="The unit group">

The set of all units <Term k="an58unitGroup">$\mathcal{O}_K^\times$</Term> forms a group under multiplication. This is the **unit group** of $\mathcal{O}_K$.

<Callout kind="question" title="Why is it a group?">
We need closure (product of units is a unit), inverses (units have inverses in $\mathcal{O}_K$), and the identity (1 is a unit). Multiplication is already associative.
</Callout>

The unit group $\mathcal{O}_K^\times$ is where the action happens. Understanding it completely will let us solve Pell's equation.
