Let's compute the norm and trace in the degree-2 field $K = \QQ(\sqrt{2})$.

<Callout kind="example" title="Norm of a + b√2">
Let $\alpha = a + b\sqrt{2} \in \QQ(\sqrt{2})$ with $b \neq 0$. Then $\alpha$ and $K$ both have degree $2$, so $k = 1$.

The two Galois conjugates of $\alpha$ are $a + b\sqrt{2}$ and $a - b\sqrt{2}$. Therefore:
$$\operatorname{N}_{K/\QQ}(\alpha) = (a + b\sqrt{2})(a - b\sqrt{2}) = a^2 - 2b^2$$
$$\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\QQ}(\alpha) = (a + b\sqrt{2}) + (a - b\sqrt{2}) = 2a$$

Nicely, these formulas also work when $b = 0$: a rational number $a$ has norm $a^2$ and trace $2a$ in $\QQ(\sqrt{2})$.
</Callout>
