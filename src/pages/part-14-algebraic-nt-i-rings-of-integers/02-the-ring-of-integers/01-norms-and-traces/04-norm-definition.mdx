<SlideLayout title="Defining norm and trace">

Let $K$ be a number field (a finite-degree extension of $\QQ$) of degree $d = [K:\QQ]$. For $\alpha \in K$, let $n = \deg \alpha$ (the degree of its minimal polynomial over $\QQ$), so $\QQ(\alpha) \subseteq K$. Set $k = d/n$ — a positive integer.

<Callout kind="definition" title="Norm and trace relative to K">
$$\operatorname{N}_{K/\QQ}(\alpha) \defeq \left(\prod_{\text{Galois conj. } \sigma} \sigma(\alpha)\right)^k$$
$$\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\QQ}(\alpha) \defeq k \cdot \left(\sum_{\text{Galois conj. } \sigma} \sigma(\alpha)\right)$$

The factor $k$ is a **correction** that accounts for elements with smaller degree than $K$ itself.
</Callout>

For a "generic" element $\alpha$ with $\QQ(\alpha) = K$ we have $k = 1$ and no correction is needed. For rational numbers like $5 \in \QQ(i)$, we get $k = 2$, giving $\operatorname{N}_{\QQ(i)/\QQ}(5) = 5^2 = 25$.
