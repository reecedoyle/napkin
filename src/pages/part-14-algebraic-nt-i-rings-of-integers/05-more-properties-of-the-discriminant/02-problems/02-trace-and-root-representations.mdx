<SlideLayout title="Problems — trace and root representations of the discriminant">

Two equivalent formulas: one replaces the embedding matrix with the trace bilinear form, the other uses the polynomial discriminant of a minimal polynomial when the ring of integers is generated by a single element.

<Problem
  client:load
  id="an57-prob-trace"
  difficulty="starred"
  prompt="Let α₁, …, αₙ be a ℤ-basis for 𝒪_K. Prove that Δ_K = det[Tr_{K/ℚ}(αᵢαⱼ)]. In particular, Δ_K is an integer."
  hint="Let M be the embedding matrix with (i,j) entry σᵢ(αⱼ). Consider the product Mᵀ · M. What is the (i,j) entry of this product? Compare with the trace."
  solution="Let M be the n×n matrix with (i,j) entry σᵢ(αⱼ). The (i,j) entry of Mᵀ · M is the dot product of column i and column j of M, which is ∑_k σ_k(αᵢ) σ_k(αⱼ) = ∑_k σ_k(αᵢαⱼ) = Tr_{K/ℚ}(αᵢαⱼ).

So det(Mᵀ M) = det[Tr_{K/ℚ}(αᵢαⱼ)]. But det(Mᵀ M) = det(Mᵀ) det(M) = det(M)² = Δ_K. Since αᵢαⱼ ∈ 𝒪_K and the trace of an algebraic integer is an integer, every entry Tr(αᵢαⱼ) is an integer. The determinant of an integer matrix is an integer, so Δ_K ∈ ℤ."
/>

<Problem
  client:load
  id="an57-prob-root"
  difficulty="starred"
  prompt="Suppose K is monogenic with 𝒪_K = ℤ[θ], and let f ∈ ℤ[x] be the minimal polynomial of θ (which is monic of degree n). The polynomial discriminant is Δ(f) = ∏_{i < j} (zᵢ − zⱼ)², where z₁, …, zₙ are the roots of f. Show that Δ_K = Δ(f)."
  hint="The basis {1, θ, θ², …, θ^{n−1}} gives an embedding matrix that is a Vandermonde matrix in the roots z₁, …, zₙ of f. Recall that the Vandermonde determinant is ∏_{i > j}(zᵢ − zⱼ)."
  solution="Take the basis {1, θ, …, θ^{n−1}} for 𝒪_K = ℤ[θ]. The j-th embedding sends θ ↦ zⱼ (the j-th root of f). The embedding matrix M has (i,j) entry σᵢ(θ^{j−1}) = zᵢ^{j−1}. This is the Vandermonde matrix V(z₁, …, zₙ).

The Vandermonde determinant formula gives det(M) = ∏_{i > j}(zᵢ − zⱼ). Squaring: Δ_K = det(M)² = ∏_{i ≠ j}(zᵢ − zⱼ) = ∏_{i < j}(zᵢ − zⱼ)² = Δ(f), as required (the leading coefficient of f is 1, so the c^{2n−2} factor is 1)."
/>
