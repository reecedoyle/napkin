<SlideLayout title="Quick examples of the discriminant">

Let us compute $\Delta_K$ for the two simplest cases.

**$K = \QQ$.** The only embedding is the identity, so the embedding matrix is the $1 \times 1$ matrix $[1]$. Thus $\Delta_\QQ = 1$.

**$K = \QQ(\sqrt{d})$, $d$ squarefree.** The two embeddings are $\sigma_1(a + b\sqrt{d}) = a + b\sqrt{d}$ and $\sigma_2(a + b\sqrt{d}) = a - b\sqrt{d}$. Take the basis $\{1, \omega\}$ for $\OO_K$, where $\omega = \frac{1+\sqrt{d}}{2}$ if $d \equiv 1 \pmod{4}$, and $\omega = \sqrt{d}$ otherwise. A short computation gives:

$$
\Delta_K =
\begin{cases}
d & \text{if } d \equiv 1 \pmod{4}, \\
4d & \text{if } d \equiv 2 \text{ or } 3 \pmod{4}.
\end{cases}
$$

<Callout kind="example" title="ℚ(√5) and ℚ(√2)">
$\Delta_{\QQ(\sqrt{5})} = 5$ (since $5 \equiv 1 \pmod 4$). $\Delta_{\QQ(\sqrt{2})} = 8$ (since $2 \equiv 2 \pmod 4$).
</Callout>
