We need a set $S$ to apply Minkowski's theorem to. Here is our "mousetrap":

<Callout kind="definition" title="Trap box S_M">
For $M > 0$, let $S$ be the set of points $(x_1, \dots, x_{r_1}, z_1, \dots, z_{r_2}) \in \mathbb{R}^{r_1} \times \mathbb{C}^{r_2}$ satisfying

$$\sum_{i=1}^{r_1} |x_i| + 2\sum_{j=1}^{r_2} |z_j| \leq M.$$

This set is convex, centrally symmetric, and compact.
</Callout>

**Why "mousetrap"?** If an element $\alpha \in \mathcal{O}_K$ has $\iota(\alpha) \in S$, then

$$\operatorname{N}_{K/\mathbb{Q}}(\alpha) = \prod_{i=1}^n |\sigma_i(\alpha)| \leq \left(\frac{M}{n}\right)^n$$

by the AM-GM inequality: the geometric mean $\leq$ arithmetic mean, and the arithmetic mean is $M/n$.

<Callout kind="lemma" title="Volume of the trap box">
Under the isomorphism $\tau \colon \mathbb{R}^{r_1} \times \mathbb{C}^{r_2} \xrightarrow{\sim} \mathbb{R}^n$, the image of $S$ has volume

$$2^{r_1} \cdot \left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right)^{r_2} \cdot \frac{M^n}{n!}.$$
</Callout>

**Sanity check for $n=2$:** signature $(2,0)$ gives $r_1=2, r_2=0$, so volume $= 4 \cdot M^2/2 = 2M^2$. This is the area of the diamond $\{(x_1,x_2): |x_1|+|x_2|\leq M\}$. ✓ Signature $(0,1)$ gives $r_1=0, r_2=1$, so volume $= (\pi/2) \cdot M^2$. This is the area of the disk $|z|\leq M/2$. ✓
