Let's build intuition with a few concrete number fields and their degrees.

<Callout kind="example" title="Quadratic, cubic, and cyclotomic fields">

| Field | Degree | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{2})$ | 2 | $\{1, \sqrt{2}\}$ |
| $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-1}) = \mathbb{Q}(i)$ | 2 | $\{1, i\}$ |
| $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt[3]{2})$ | 3 | $\{1, \sqrt[3]{2}, \sqrt[3]{4}\}$ |
| $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_5)$, where $\zeta_5 = e^{2\pi i/5}$ | 4 | $\{1, \zeta_5, \zeta_5^2, \zeta_5^3\}$ |

The degree 2 case is called a **quadratic field**. Any $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{d})$ for squarefree $d \neq 1$ is quadratic.

</Callout>
