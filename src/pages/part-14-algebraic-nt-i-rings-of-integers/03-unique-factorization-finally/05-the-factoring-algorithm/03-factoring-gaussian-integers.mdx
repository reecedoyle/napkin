Let $K = \QQ(i)$, so $\OO_K = \ZZ[i]$ and $f = x^2 + 1$. Let us factor $(29)$ in $\ZZ[i]$.

We need to factor $x^2 + 1$ in $\FF_{29}[x]$. We look for $c$ with $c^2 \equiv -1 \pmod{29}$.

Since $29 \equiv 1 \pmod 4$, $-1$ is a square mod $29$. In fact $12^2 = 144 = 5 \cdot 29 - 1 \equiv -1 \pmod{29}$.

So $x^2 + 1 \equiv (x - 12)(x + 12) \pmod{29}$, giving two distinct irreducible factors. By the Dedekind–Kummer theorem:

$$(29) = (29, i - 12)(29, i + 12).$$

Both factors are prime ideals. We can simplify: in $\ZZ[i]$, the ideal $(29, i-12) = (5 - 2i)$ because $\Norm(5-2i) = 25 + 4 = 29$ (a prime norm means the element is a Gaussian prime).
