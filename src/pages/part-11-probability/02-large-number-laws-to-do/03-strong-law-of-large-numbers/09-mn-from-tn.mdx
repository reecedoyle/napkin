<SlideLayout title="From Tₙ convergence to Mₙ → 0">

We have shown $T_n$ converges in almost every world. Now we extract the SLLN.

<Callout kind="proposition" title="Relation between Tₙ and Mₙ">
In every world where $T_n$ converges, $M_n \to 0$.
</Callout>

Note that $X_n = n(T_n - T_{n-1})$. So:
$$M_n = \frac{X_1 + 2(T_2 - T_1) + 3(T_3 - T_2) + \cdots + n(T_n - T_{n-1})}{n}.$$

A summation-by-parts (Abel's) calculation gives:
$$M_n = T_n - \frac{T_1 + T_2 + \cdots + T_{n-1}}{n}.$$

If $T_n \to L$, then the <Term k="llCesaro">Cesàro mean</Term> $(T_1 + \cdots + T_{n-1})/n$ also $\to L$.

Therefore $M_n = T_n - \frac{T_1 + \cdots + T_{n-1}}{n} \to L - L = 0$. $\square$

This completes the proof of the strong law of large numbers under the finite-variance assumption.

</SlideLayout>
