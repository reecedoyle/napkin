<SlideLayout title="The weighted series Tₙ">

The finite-variance proof proceeds via a clever auxiliary series.

<Callout kind="definition" title="The weighted partial-sum series">
Define the <Term k="llWeightedSeries">weighted series</Term>
$$T_n = X_1 + \frac{X_2}{2} + \frac{X_3}{3} + \cdots + \frac{X_n}{n}.$$
</Callout>

This step is hard to motivate up front — think of it as: "scale down later terms to keep the total variance finite."

<Callout kind="proposition" title="Key claim">
In almost every world, the sequence $T_n$ converges (as $n \to \infty$).
</Callout>

**Why finite variance helps:** the individual variances are
$$\Var\!\left[\frac{X_n}{n}\right] = \frac{\Var[X_1]}{n^2}.$$

So the total variance of all terms is
$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \Var\!\left[\frac{X_n}{n}\right] = \Var[X_1] \cdot \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^2} = \Var[X_1] \cdot \frac{\pi^2}{6} < \infty.$$

Finite total variance is the engine of the proof. The next slide shows how to convert this into almost sure convergence.
