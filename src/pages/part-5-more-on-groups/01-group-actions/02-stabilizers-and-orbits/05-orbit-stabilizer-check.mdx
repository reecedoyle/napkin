<SlideLayout title="Using orbit-stabilizer — a quick check">

The orbit-stabilizer theorem gives us $|G| = |\OO| \cdot |\Stab_G(x)|$ whenever $G$ acts on $X$ and $\OO$ is the orbit of $x$.

<Callout kind="example" title="Setup">
The <Term k="Sn">symmetric group $S_4$</Term> acts on the set $\{1,2,3,4\}$ by applying permutations. Consider the point $x = 1$.

- The orbit of $1$ is all of $\{1,2,3,4\}$ (any element can be sent to any other), so $|\OO_1| = 4$.
- By orbit-stabilizer, $|{\Stab}_{S_4}(1)| = |S_4|/|\OO_1| = 24/4 = 6$.
</Callout>
