<Callout kind="definition" title="Group action">
Let $X$ be a set and $G$ a group. A <Term k="groupAction">**group action**</Term> is a binary operation
$$\cdot \colon G \times X \to X$$
satisfying two axioms for all $g_1, g_2 \in G$ and $x \in X$:
1. **Compatibility**: $(g_1 g_2) \cdot x = g_1 \cdot (g_2 \cdot x)$.
2. **Identity**: $1_G \cdot x = x$.
</Callout>

Compatibility says "apply $g_2$ then $g_1$" equals "apply the product $g_1 g_2$ at once". The identity axiom says the neutral element does nothing.

Think of the action as a family of bijections $\phi_g \colon X \to X$ (one for each $g \in G$) that compose in the same way as the group elements do: $\phi_{g_1 g_2} = \phi_{g_1} \circ \phi_{g_2}$.
