<SlideLayout title="Composition series">

Every integer factors as a product of primes. The analogous statement for groups uses normal subgroups.

<Callout kind="definition" title="Composition series">
A <Term k="compositionSeries">**composition series**</Term> of a group $G$ is a chain of subgroups of maximal length:
$$\{1\} = H_0 \trianglelefteq H_1 \trianglelefteq H_2 \trianglelefteq \cdots \trianglelefteq H_n = G.$$
The <Term k="compositionFactors">**composition factors**</Term> are the quotient groups $H_1/H_0,\, H_2/H_1,\, \dots,\, H_n/H_{n-1}$.
</Callout>

The "maximal length" condition forces each composition factor to be a <Term k="simpleGroup">simple group</Term> — if $H_{i+1}/H_i$ were not simple, we could insert another group between $H_i$ and $H_{i+1}$, contradicting maximality.

**Example.** For $\Zc{12}$:
$$\{0\} \trianglelefteq \Zc{2} \trianglelefteq \Zc{4} \trianglelefteq \Zc{12}$$
has composition factors $\Zc{2}$, $\Zc{2}$, $\Zc{3}$ — mirroring the prime factorisation $12 = 2^2 \cdot 3$.

</SlideLayout>
