<Callout kind="definition" title="Rank">
The <Term k="abelianRank">**rank**</Term> of a <Term k="fgAbelian">finitely generated abelian group</Term> $G$ is the integer $r$ appearing in the decomposition
$$G \cong \ZZ^{\oplus r} \oplus \Zc{q_1} \oplus \dots \oplus \Zc{q_m}.$$
</Callout>

The rank measures the "free" part of $G$. Groups with rank 0 are finite (they are products of cyclic groups of prime-power order). Groups with rank $> 0$ are infinite.
