A key structural fact underlies the derived series.

<Callout kind="theorem" title="Commutator subgroup facts">
For any group $G$, the commutator subgroup $[G, G]$ is normal in $G$, and the quotient $G / [G, G]$ is abelian. Moreover, $[G, G]$ is the **smallest** normal subgroup with abelian quotient.
</Callout>

The normality is easy: for any $g \in G$ and commutator $[a, b] = aba^{-1}b^{-1}$, we have
$$g[a, b]g^{-1} = (gag^{-1})(gbg^{-1})(gag^{-1})^{-1}(gbg^{-1})^{-1} = [gag^{-1}, gbg^{-1}],$$
which is again a commutator (with conjugated elements). So conjugation preserves the set of commutators, meaning $[G, G]$ is closed under conjugation — it's normal.
