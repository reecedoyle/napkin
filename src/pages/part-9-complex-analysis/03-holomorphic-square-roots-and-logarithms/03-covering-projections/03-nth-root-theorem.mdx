We can now state and prove the main result of this section.

<Callout kind="theorem" title="Existence of holomorphic nth roots">
Let $f \colon U \to \CC$ be holomorphic. Then $f$ has a <Term k="holomorphicNthRoot">holomorphic $n$th root</Term> if and only if
$$\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_\gamma \frac{f'}{f}\,dz \in n\ZZ$$
for every closed <Term k="contour">contour</Term> $\gamma$ in $U$.
</Callout>

  solution="The squaring map (and more generally the nth power map) on ℂ \\ {0} is a covering projection of degree n. The fundamental group of the punctured plane is ℤ, generated by a single loop around the origin. Under the nth power map, this generator maps to n times the generator, so the image of π₁ in the base is nℤ. By the lifting theorem, a continuous (hence holomorphic) lift exists iff f pushes every loop in U into nℤ — i.e., the winding number of f around 0 is divisible by n for every loop. The integral (1/2πi)∮_γ f'/f dz computes this winding number."
