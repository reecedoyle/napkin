<SlideLayout title="π₁ and even winding">

Both the total space $E = \CC \setminus \{0\}$ and the base $B = \CC \setminus \{0\}$ have <Term k="fundamentalGroup">fundamental group</Term> $\pi_1 \cong \ZZ$ (generated by a counterclockwise loop around the origin).

Under the squaring map $p = (-)^2$, a loop that winds once around $0$ in $E$ maps to a loop that winds **twice** around $0$ in $B$. So

$$p_*(\pi_1(E)) = 2\ZZ \subseteq \ZZ = \pi_1(B).$$

<Callout kind="corollary" title="Reformulation in terms of winding numbers">
A holomorphic square root of $f$ exists if and only if, for every loop $\gamma$ in $V$, the curve $f \circ \gamma$ winds an **even** number of times around $0$ in $B$. This is the condition
$$\frac{1}{2\pi i} \oint_\gamma \frac{f'}{f}\,dz \in 2\ZZ.$$
</Callout>

Replacing $2$ by $n$ throughout gives the theorem for $n$th roots. The image of $p_*(\pi_1(E))$ for $p = (-)^n$ is $n\ZZ \subseteq \ZZ$, and the existence condition becomes divisibility by $n$.

</SlideLayout>
