<SlideLayout title="Understanding liminf for functions">

The pointwise <Term k="liminf">$\liminf$</Term> of a sequence of functions is defined by
applying the scalar $\liminf$ at each point:

$$\left(\liminf_{n \to \infty} f_n\right)(\omega) \;=\; \liminf_{n \to \infty} f_n(\omega).$$

Recall: $\liminf_{n\to\infty} a_n = \lim_{n\to\infty} \inf_{k \ge n} a_k$ — the largest
value the sequence eventually stays above.
