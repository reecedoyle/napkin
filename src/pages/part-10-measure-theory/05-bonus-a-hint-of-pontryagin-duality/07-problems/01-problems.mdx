  id="pont-prob-compact-iff-discrete"
  difficulty="standard"
  prompt="Show that an LCA group $G$ is compact if and only if $\widehat{G}$ is discrete. (You will need the compact-open topology on $\widehat{G}$.)"
  hint="After applying Pontryagin duality, it suffices to show: (1) G compact implies G-hat discrete, and (2) G discrete implies G-hat compact. Both are topological arguments using the compact-open topology — no heavy machinery needed."
  solution="For (1): assume G is compact. In the compact-open topology on G-hat, the trivial character is isolated because the set {ξ : ξ(G) ⊆ V} for a small enough arc V around 1 ∈ 𝕋 contains only the trivial character (any continuous homomorphism from the compact G into a small arc of 𝕋 must be trivial). So every point of G-hat is isolated, meaning G-hat is discrete. For (2): assume G is discrete. Then G-hat = Hom(G, 𝕋) is a closed subset of 𝕋^G. By Tychonoff's theorem 𝕋^G is compact, and G-hat is a closed subgroup, hence compact."
