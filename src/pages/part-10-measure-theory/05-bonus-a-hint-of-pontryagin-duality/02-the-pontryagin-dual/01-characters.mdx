<SlideLayout title="Characters of an LCA group">

In Fourier analysis on $\RR$, the building blocks are the complex exponentials $x \mapsto e^{2\pi i \xi x}$. Each one is a continuous group homomorphism $\RR \to \TT$. We abstract this.

<Callout kind="definition" title="Character">
Let $G$ be an LCA group. A <Term k="character">**character**</Term> of $G$ is a continuous group homomorphism $\xi \colon G \to \TT$.
</Callout>

A character $\xi$ respects both the group structure ($\xi(x+y) = \xi(x)\xi(y)$) and the topology (continuity). The values live in the circle <Term k="circleGroup">$\TT$</Term>, so characters are "oscillatory" functions on $G$.

<Callout kind="definition" title="Pontryagin dual">
The <Term k="pontryaginDual">**Pontryagin dual**</Term> of an LCA group $G$ is the abelian group
$$\wh G \defeq \{\text{continuous group homomorphisms } \xi \colon G \to \TT\}$$
with pointwise multiplication $(\xi_1 \xi_2)(x) = \xi_1(x)\xi_2(x)$.
</Callout>

With the compact-open topology, $\wh G$ is itself an LCA group — so the duality operation produces a new LCA group from any LCA group.

</SlideLayout>
