<SlideLayout title="The three-step construction table">

Putting everything together so far, we have a three-step pipeline from pre-measure to complete measure:

| Domain | Function | Step | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| $2^\Omega$ | $\mu^\ast$ | Step 2 | Outer measure obtained from $\mu_0$ |
| $\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{cm}}$ | $\mu^{\mathrm{cm}}$ | Step 3 | Complete measure on Carathéodory-measurable sets |
| $\mathcal{A}_0$ | $\mu_0$ | Step 1 | Pre-measure on algebra |

Going **down** a row corresponds to restriction (to a smaller domain). Going **up** corresponds to extension. The steps are numbered in construction order: we define $\mu_0$ first, build $\mu^\ast$ second, then identify $\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{cm}}$ third.

The two compatibility results together tell us $\mathcal{A}^{\mathrm{cm}} \supseteq \mathcal{A}_0$ and $\mu^{\mathrm{cm}}$ agrees with $\mu_0$ on $\mathcal{A}_0$: the construction truly extends the original.
