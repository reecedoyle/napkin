<Callout kind="remark" title="Moral">
Frobenius elements upstairs restrict to Frobenius elements downstairs.
</Callout>

This might sound like a small technical fact, but it's the load-bearing wall for everything left in the chapter. Both the quadratic reciprocity proof and the general "Frobenius controls factorization" theorem work by picking a Frobenius element way up in a big splitting field, and then restricting it down to read off information about a smaller subfield we actually care about.
