<SlideLayout title="Step 1: set up the cyclotomic field">

For this proof, fix $L = \QQ(\zeta_q)$, where $\zeta_q$ is a primitive $q$-th root of unity. Then $L/\QQ$ is Galois, with Galois group $G$.

<Callout kind="question" title="A unique index-two subgroup">
Show that $G$ has a unique subgroup $H$ of index two.
</Callout>

<ProofReveal
  client:load
  id="frob-qr-proof-unique-index-two"
  prompt="Show that G = Gal(L/ℚ) has a unique subgroup H of index two."
  solution="G ≅ (ℤ/qℤ)^× ≅ ℤ/(q−1)ℤ is cyclic of order q−1, which is even since q is an odd prime. A cyclic group of even order 2m has a unique subgroup of index 2, namely the unique subgroup of order m (cyclic groups have exactly one subgroup of each order dividing the group order). Concretely, viewing G ≅ ℤ/qℤ via σ_n ↔ n, H is the subgroup of squares mod q, i.e. H = {σ_n : n is a quadratic residue mod q}."
/>

We can describe $H$ exactly: viewing $G \cong \Zm q$, we have <Term k="frob61QuadraticSubfield">$H = \{\sigma_n \mid n \text{ a quadratic residue mod } q\}$</Term>.
