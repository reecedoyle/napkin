<Callout kind="theorem" title="The Frobenius element">
Assume $K/\QQ$ is Galois with Galois group $G$. Let $p$ be a rational prime unramified in $K$, and $\kp$ a prime above it. There is a **unique** element $\Frob_\kp \in G$ with the property that, for all $\alpha \in \OO_K$,
$$
\Frob_\kp(\alpha) \equiv \alpha^{p} \pmod{\kp}.
$$
It is called the <Term k="frob61FrobeniusElement">Frobenius element</Term> at $\kp$, and has order $f$.
</Callout>

The **uniqueness** part is pretty important: it means we can show a given $\sigma \in \Gal(K/\QQ)$ is the Frobenius element just by observing that it satisfies the functional equation above — we never have to reconstruct $\Frob_\kp$ from scratch, only verify a guess.
