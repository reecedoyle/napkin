Combining the cyclotomic Frobenius lemma with our earlier order lemma (order of $\Frob_\kp$ = inertial degree, and $\Frob_\kp = \id$ iff $p$ <Term k="frob61SplitsCompletely">splits completely</Term>) gives a clean numerical criterion.

<Callout kind="question" title="Conclude">
For which rational primes $p$ does $p$ split completely in $\OO_L = \ZZ[\zeta_q]$?
</Callout>

  solution="By the order lemma, p splits completely in 𝒪_L exactly when Frob_𝔭 = id for 𝔭 above p. By the cyclotomic Frobenius lemma, Frob_𝔭 = σ_p. So p splits completely iff σ_p = id, i.e. iff σ_p fixes ζ_q, i.e. iff ζ_q^p = ζ_q, i.e. iff p ≡ 1 (mod q)."
