<Callout kind="theorem" title="Frobenius elements control decomposition">
Let $\QQ \subseteq E \subseteq K$ be an extension of number fields, with $K/\QQ$ Galois (though $E/\QQ$ need not be). Pick an unramified rational prime $p$; let $G = \Gal(K/\QQ)$ and $H$ the corresponding intermediate subgroup. Finally, let $\kP$ be a prime above $p$ in $K$.

Then the **factorization pattern** of $p$ in $E$ equals the **cycle structure** of $\Frob_{\kP}$ acting on the left cosets of $H$.
</Callout>

Often we take $E = K$ itself, in which case this is just saying: the decomposition of $p$ in $K$ is controlled by a single Frobenius element sitting above it.
