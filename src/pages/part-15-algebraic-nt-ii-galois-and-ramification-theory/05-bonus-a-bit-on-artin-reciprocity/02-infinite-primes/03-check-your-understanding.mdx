<MCQ

  correct="b"
  explanation="ℚ(√-5) has degree 2 with no real embeddings — complex conjugation is the only nontrivial automorphism, and neither embedding lands inside ℝ. So it has a single complex infinite prime and no real ones: totally complex. ℚ(√5), ℚ, and ℚ(√2) are all totally real (every embedding of a real quadratic field, or of ℚ itself, lands in ℝ)."
