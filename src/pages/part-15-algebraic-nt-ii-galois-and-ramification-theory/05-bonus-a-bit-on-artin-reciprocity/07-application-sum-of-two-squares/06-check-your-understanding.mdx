Let's use the final theorem: $p = x^2+5y^2$ for a prime $p \nmid 20$ if and only if $p \bmod 20 \in \{1,9\}$.

<Callout kind="example" title="Sanity check">
$p = 29$: since $29 \bmod 20 = 9$, the theorem predicts $29$ is expressible as $x^2+5y^2$. Indeed $29 = 3^2 + 5\cdot 2^2$.
</Callout>
