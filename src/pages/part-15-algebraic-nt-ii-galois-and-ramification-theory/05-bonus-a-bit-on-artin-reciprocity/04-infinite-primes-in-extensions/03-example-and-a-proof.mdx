<SlideLayout title="Example: ∞ ramifying (or not)">

Let $\infty$ be the real infinite prime of $\QQ$.

<Callout kind="example" title="Ramified vs. unramified">
- $\infty$ **ramifies** in $\QQ(\sqrt{-5})/\QQ$: the single embedding $\QQ \to \RR$ extends only to complex embeddings of $\QQ(\sqrt{-5})$ (since $\sqrt{-5} \notin \RR$).
- $\infty$ is **unramified** in $\QQ(\sqrt{5})/\QQ$: it extends to two *real* embeddings of $\QQ(\sqrt{5})$ (both $\sqrt5 \mapsto \pm\sqrt5 \in \RR$).
</Callout>
