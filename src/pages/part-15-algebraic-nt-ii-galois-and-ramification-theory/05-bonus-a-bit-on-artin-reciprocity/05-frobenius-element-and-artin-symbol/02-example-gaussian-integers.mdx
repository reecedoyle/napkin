Let $L = \QQ(i)$, $K = \QQ$, so $\Gal(L/K) \cong \Zc{2}$.

For an odd prime $p$ with $\kP$ above it, $\Frob_\kP$ is the unique element such that $(a+bi)^p \equiv \Frob_\kP(a+bi) \pmod \kP$ in $\ZZ[i]$. In particular,

$$
\Frob_\kP(i) = i^p = \begin{cases} i & p \equiv 1 \pmod 4 \\ -i & p \equiv 3 \pmod 4. \end{cases}
$$

So $\Frob_\kP$ is the identity when $p \equiv 1 \pmod 4$, and complex conjugation when $p \equiv 3 \pmod 4$.
