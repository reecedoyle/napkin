<Callout kind="lemma" title="Finite fields have prime power orders">

Let $F$ be a finite field. Then:

- (a) Its characteristic is nonzero, hence some prime $p$.
- (b) $F$ is a finite extension of $\mathbb{F}_p$, and in particular an $\mathbb{F}_p$-<Term k="vectorSpace">vector space</Term>.
- (c) $|F| = p^n$ for some prime $p$ and integer $n$.

</Callout>

We already showed the characteristic can't be a composite number two slides ago. Combined with the pigeonhole principle (only finitely many elements to work with), that forces the characteristic to be some prime $p$. The rest of the lemma follows from treating $F$ as a vector space — that's the proof on the next slide.
