<SlideLayout title="The complete classification of finite fields">

Here's the "main surprise" of this chapter: for each allowed size $p^n$, there's only **one** finite field, up to isomorphism.

<Callout kind="theorem" title="Complete classification of finite fields">
A field $F$ is a finite field with $p^n$ elements if and only if it is a <Term k="ff59SplittingField">splitting field</Term> of $x^{p^n} - x$ over $\mathbb{F}_p$.
</Callout>

Splitting fields of a fixed polynomial over a fixed base field are always isomorphic to each other (a fact from general field theory) — so this theorem immediately gives us fact #3 from the chapter overview: any two finite fields of the same order $p^n$ are isomorphic.
