<SlideLayout title="The Frobenius automorphism">

We exhibit an explicit element of the Galois group: the "raise everything to the $p$-th power" map.

<Callout kind="theorem" title="The p-th power automorphism">
The map $\sigma_p \colon \mathbb{F}_{p^n} \to \mathbb{F}_{p^n}$ defined by $\sigma_p(x) = x^p$ is a <Term k="ff59Automorphism">field automorphism</Term>, and moreover it fixes $\mathbb{F}_p$ pointwise.
</Callout>

This map is called the <Term k="ff59Frobenius">Frobenius automorphism</Term>. It will reappear later, in a much more general setting, whenever a field has characteristic $p$. For now, let's just check it does what we claim.

</SlideLayout>
