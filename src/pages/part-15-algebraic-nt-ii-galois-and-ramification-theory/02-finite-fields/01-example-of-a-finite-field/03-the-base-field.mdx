The simplest finite fields are the ones you already know.

<Callout kind="example" title="Base field">
Let <Term k="finiteField">$\mathbb{F}_p$</Term> denote the field of integers modulo a prime $p$. This is a field with $p$ elements, and characteristic $p$: adding $1$ to itself $p$ times gives $p \equiv 0 \pmod p$.
</Callout>

Every finite field will turn out to contain a copy of some $\mathbb{F}_p$ sitting inside it — that's the content of the next section. But finite fields can be much bigger than $p$ elements while still having characteristic $p$, as the next example shows.
