<SlideLayout title="The characteristic of the field of nine elements">

Although $F$ (our field of nine elements) has $9$ elements, every element $x \in F$ satisfies

$$
3x = \underbrace{x + \dots + x}_{3 \text{ times}} = 0.
$$

In particular, $F$ has characteristic $3$ — the *size* of the field ($9$) and its *characteristic* ($3$) are different numbers, and that gap is exactly $p^n$ versus $p$.
