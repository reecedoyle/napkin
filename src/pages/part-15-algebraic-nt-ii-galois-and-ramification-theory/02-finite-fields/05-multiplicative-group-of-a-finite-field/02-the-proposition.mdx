<Callout kind="proposition" title="The multiplicative group of a finite field is cyclic">
Let $F$ be a finite field. Then the multiplicative group <Term k="ff59MultGroupCyclic">$F^\times$</Term> is a cyclic group.
</Callout>

This says $F^\times$ is as simple as an abelian group of that size can be: it's isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}/(p^n-1)\mathbb{Z}$, generated by a single element. There's no analogue of this for the *additive* group $(F,+)$ in general — it's the multiplicative structure that's forced to be cyclic.

</SlideLayout>
