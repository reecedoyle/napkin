This last section proves a fact that isn't needed for the rest of the book, but is too pretty to skip. Consider the field $F$ of order $p = 17$, and ask:

- How many nonzero elements of $F$ are <Term k="ff59QuadraticResidue">quadratic residues</Term> (squares of another element)?
- Is there an element $x \in F$ with $x^2 = -1$?

Both questions are answerable by brute-force computation with only 16 nonzero elements. But there's a structural fact that makes both questions easy *without* any computation at all.
