<Callout kind="example" title="The multiplicative group of 𝔽_17">

$F^\times$ is cyclic of order $16$, so there's some generator $g \in F^\times$ with every nonzero element equal to $g^0 = 1, g^1, g^2, \dots, g^{15}$. Squaring every nonzero element gives (using $g^{16} = g^0 = 1$)
$$
g^0, g^2, g^4, \dots, g^{14}, \; g^0, g^2, \dots, g^{14}.
$$

</Callout>

Each even exponent $g^0, g^2, \dots, g^{14}$ shows up exactly twice among the sixteen squares — so the *distinct* quadratic residues are just $g^0, g^2, g^4, \dots, g^{14}$, that is, half of $F^\times$.
