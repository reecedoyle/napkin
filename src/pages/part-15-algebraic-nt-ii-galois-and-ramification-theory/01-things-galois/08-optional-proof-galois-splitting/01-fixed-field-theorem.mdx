<SlideLayout title="(Optional) The fixed field theorem">

This section proves the theorem from §5 that $|\Aut(K/F)|$ divides $[K:F]$, with equality exactly when $K$ is a splitting field. It's optional — feel free to skip ahead if you're happy taking that theorem on faith.

We start by extracting a useful fragment.

<Callout kind="theorem" title="Fixed field theorem">
Let $K$ be a field and $G$ a subgroup of $\Aut(K)$. Then $[K:K^G] = |G|$.
</Callout>

This says the fixed-field construction from §6 is "sharp": the degree of $K$ over the fixed field of $G$ is exactly the size of $G$, not merely bounded by it.
