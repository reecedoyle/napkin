Let's reprise the §1 examples using $\Aut(K/\QQ)$.

<Callout kind="example" title="Automorphism groups, revisited">
- $\Aut(\QQ(i)/\QQ) \cong \Zc2$, with elements $z \mapsto z$ and $z \mapsto \ol z$.
- $\Aut(\QQ(\sqrt2)/\QQ) \cong \Zc2$, the same way.
- $\Aut(\QQ(\cbrt2)/\QQ)$ is the **trivial group** — only the identity.
</Callout>

The first two match what we found for embeddings into $\CC$ in §1, since every embedding of $\QQ(i)$ or $\QQ(\sqrt2)$ happened to land back inside the field. But $\QQ(\cbrt2)$ had three embeddings into $\CC$, and only one of them (the identity) maps $\QQ(\cbrt2)$ back into itself.
