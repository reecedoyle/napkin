<SlideLayout title="The n embeddings theorem, generalized">

With separability in hand, the §3 theorem generalizes verbatim to any base field.

<Callout kind="theorem" title="The n embeddings of any separable extension">
Let $K/F$ be a separable extension of degree $n$, and let $\ol F$ be an algebraic closure of $F$. Then there are exactly $n$ field homomorphisms $K \injto \ol F$, say $\sigma_1, \dots, \sigma_n$, which fix $F$.
</Callout>

And trace and norm follow along too, for any separable extension:

<Callout kind="definition" title="Trace and norm for K/F">
Let $K/F$ be separable of degree $n$ with embeddings $\sigma_1, \dots, \sigma_n$ into $\ol F$. Define
$$
\Tr_{K/F}(\alpha) = \sum_{i=1}^n \sigma_i(\alpha)
\quad\text{and}\quad
\Norm_{K/F}(\alpha) = \prod_{i=1}^n \sigma_i(\alpha).
$$
When $F = \QQ$ and $\ol F = \CC$, this is exactly the definition from §3.
</Callout>
