Let's compute some fixed fields for our running example. Take $K = \QQ(\sqrt2,\sqrt3)$ with $G = \Gal(K/\QQ) = \{\id,\sigma,\tau,\sigma\tau\}$, the Klein four group, where $\sigma(\sqrt2) = -\sqrt2$, $\sigma(\sqrt3) = \sqrt3$, and $\tau$ goes the other way ($\tau(\sqrt2)=\sqrt2$, $\tau(\sqrt3)=-\sqrt3$).
