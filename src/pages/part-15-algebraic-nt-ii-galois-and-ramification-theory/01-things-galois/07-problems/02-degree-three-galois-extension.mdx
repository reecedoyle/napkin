Since Gal(ℚ(ζ₇)/ℚ) ≅ (ℤ/7ℤ)ˣ is cyclic of order 6, and complex conjugation is the unique element of order 2 in this cyclic group, K is the fixed field of that order-2 subgroup. By the fundamental theorem, [K:ℚ] = 6/2 = 3, and because the ambient group is cyclic (hence every subgroup is normal), K/ℚ is itself Galois, with Galois group cyclic of order 3."
