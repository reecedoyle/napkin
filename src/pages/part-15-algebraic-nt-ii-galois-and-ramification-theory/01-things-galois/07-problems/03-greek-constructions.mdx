(b) Squaring the circle needs √π, and since π is transcendental, √π is transcendental too — infinite degree, certainly not a power of 2. (c) Trisecting a 60° angle needs cos(20°), a root of the irreducible cubic 8x³−6x−1, again degree 3. In every case the degree obstruction rules out the construction."
