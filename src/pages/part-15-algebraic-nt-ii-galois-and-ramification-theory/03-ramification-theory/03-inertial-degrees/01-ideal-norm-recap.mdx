<SlideLayout title="Recall the ideal norm">

We already have a tool measuring how "roomy" an ideal is: the <Term k="an55idealNorm">ideal norm</Term> $\Norm(\ka) = \left\lvert \OO_K / \ka \right\rvert$.

For example, $(5)$ has ideal norm $5^2 = 25$ in $\ZZ[i]$, since

$$
\ZZ[i]/(5) \cong \left\{ a+bi \mid a,b \in \Zc5 \right\}
$$

has $5^2 = 25$ elements.

We're going to use the norm to pin down exactly how each prime $\kp_i$ above $p$ "uses up" the $n$ dimensions of $K$.
