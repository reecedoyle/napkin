Let's test the pattern on a specific prime. Since $13 = 4 + 9 = 2^2 + 3^2$, we can write

$$
13 = (2+3i)(2-3i)
$$

in $\ZZ[i]$, and one can check $(2+3i)$ and $(2-3i)$ are non-associate primes of $\ZZ[i]$.
