<SlideLayout title="θ is onto, with kernel of order e">

Surprisingly, *every* automorphism of the residue field extension arises this way.

<Callout kind="theorem" title="Decomposition group and Galois group">
With $\theta: D_\kp \to \Gal\left( (\OO_K/\kp)/\FF_p \right)$ as above:
- $\theta$ is **surjective**, and
- its kernel has order $e$, the ramification index.

In particular, if $p$ is unramified then $D_\kp \cong \Gal\left( (\OO_K/\kp)/\FF_p \right)$.
</Callout>

(The proof is not hard but a bit long, and not very enlightening — we'll skip it.)

Now, we already know what the target group is! Since $\OO_K/\kp \cong \FF_{p^f}$,

$$
\Gal\left( (\OO_K/\kp)/\FF_p \right) \cong \Gal(\FF_{p^f}/\FF_p) \cong \left< x \mapsto x^p \right> \cong \Zc f.
$$

So $D_\kp \cong \Zc f$ as well — the decomposition group is always cyclic of order $f$.

</SlideLayout>
