<Callout kind="theorem" title="Discriminant detects ramification">
Let $p$ be a rational prime and $K$ a number field. Then $p$ is ramified in $\OO_K$ if and only if $p \mid \Delta_K$.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="example" title="Ramification in the Gaussian integers">
Let $K = \QQ(i)$, so $\OO_K = \ZZ[i]$ and $\Delta_K = -4$. As predicted, the only prime ramifying in $\ZZ[i]$ is $2$ — the only prime factor of $\Delta_K$.
</Callout>

Since $\Delta_K$ is a fixed nonzero integer with only finitely many prime factors, we get a nice corollary for free:

<Callout kind="remark" title="Only finitely many primes ramify">
For any number field $K$, only finitely many rational primes ramify in $\OO_K$ — namely, the prime factors of $\Delta_K$.
</Callout>
