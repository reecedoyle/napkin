<SlideLayout title="The efg theorem">

<Callout kind="theorem" title="Inertial degrees and ramification indices are all equal">
Assume $K/\QQ$ is Galois. Then for any rational prime $p$,

$$
p \cdot \OO_K = \left( \kp_1 \kp_2 \cdots \kp_g \right)^e
$$

for some single $e$, where the $\kp_i$ are distinct primes with the *same* inertial degree $f$. Hence $[K:\QQ] = efg$.
</Callout>

**Why the $f_i$ are all equal:** each $\sigma \in G$ induces a ring isomorphism $\OO_K/\kp \cong \OO_K/\sigma(\kp)$ (just reduce $\sigma$ mod $\kp$). Since these two quotients are isomorphic, they have the same size, so $\kp$ and $\sigma(\kp)$ have the same inertial degree. Because the action is transitive, *every* $\kp_i$ is $\sigma(\kp_1)$ for some $\sigma$ — so all the $f_i$ agree.
