<SlideLayout title="Beyond ℚ — general Galois extensions">

Everything in this chapter carries over verbatim if we replace $K/\QQ$ by any Galois extension $L/F$ of number fields. Instead of a rational prime $p$ breaking down in $\OO_K$, we have a prime ideal $\kp$ of $F$ breaking down as

$$
\kp \cdot \OO_L = \left( \kP_1 \cdots \kP_g \right)^e
$$

in $\OO_L$, where the $\kP_i$ are the primes of $L$ above $\kp$. Every result — the efg theorem, transitivity of the Galois action, the decomposition and inertia groups — holds with $\FF_p$ replaced by the residue field $\OO_F/\kp$.

<Callout kind="remark" title="Why we stuck with ℚ">
The only reason this chapter worked with $F=\QQ$ throughout is that capital Gothic $P$'s ($\kP$) look *really* terrifying.
</Callout>
