<Callout kind="theorem" title="Frobenius determinant">
For any finite group $G$, the polynomial $\det M_G$ factors into irreducible polynomials such that:
1. The **number** of irreducible factors equals the number of <Term k="conjugacyClass">conjugacy classes</Term> of $G$, and
2. The **multiplicity** of each irreducible factor equals its degree.
</Callout>

Equivalently: if $V_1, \dots, V_r$ are the distinct <Term k="complexIrrep">complex irreps</Term> of $G$ and $d_i = \dim V_i$, then
$$\det M_G = \prod_{i=1}^r (F_i)^{d_i}$$
where each $F_i$ is an irreducible polynomial of degree $d_i$.

<Callout kind="remark" title="Connection to sum of squares">
Taking degrees: $\sum_i d_i \cdot d_i = \sum_i d_i^2 = |G|$. This is exactly the sum-of-squares identity for representations!
</Callout>
