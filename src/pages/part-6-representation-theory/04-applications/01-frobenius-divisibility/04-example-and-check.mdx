<SlideLayout title="Frobenius divisibility — checking it">

Frobenius divisibility gives an easy sanity-check: for any finite group $G$, the dimensions of all <Term k="complexIrrep">complex irreps</Term> must divide $|G|$.

<Callout kind="example" title="S₃, order 6">
$S_3$ has three conjugacy classes, so three irreps. Their dimensions satisfy $d_1^2 + d_2^2 + d_3^2 = |S_3| = 6$. The only solution with each $d_i \mid 6$ is $d_i = 1, 1, 2$.
</Callout>
