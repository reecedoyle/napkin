<Callout kind="theorem" title="Characterisation of semisimple algebras">
Let $A$ be a finite-dimensional algebra. The following are equivalent:

1. $A \cong \bigoplus_i \Mat_{d_i}(k)$ for some positive integers $d_i$.
2. $A$ is <Term k="semisimpleAlgebra">semisimple</Term>.
3. The <Term k="regularRep">regular representation</Term> $\Reg(A)$ is completely reducible.
</Callout>

The chain is: (i) $\Rightarrow$ (ii) $\Rightarrow$ (iii) $\Rightarrow$ (i).

**(i) $\Rightarrow$ (ii):** Every rep of $\bigoplus_i \Mat_{d_i}(k)$ decomposes into reps of each $\Mat_{d_i}(k)$, which are all completely reducible.

**(ii) $\Rightarrow$ (iii):** Tautological — the regular rep is a representation.

**(iii) $\Rightarrow$ (i):** The clever step. Write $\Reg(A) = \bigoplus_i V_i^{\oplus n_i}$. Then $\Homrep(\Reg(A), \Reg(A)) \cong \bigoplus_i \Mat_{n_i}(k)$ by Schur's theorem. But also $\Homrep(\Reg(A), \Reg(A)) \cong A^{\mathrm{op}}$ (right multiplication). Since $\Mat_n(k)^{\mathrm{op}} \cong \Mat_n(k)$ (by transpose), we get $A \cong \bigoplus_i \Mat_{n_i}(k)$.
