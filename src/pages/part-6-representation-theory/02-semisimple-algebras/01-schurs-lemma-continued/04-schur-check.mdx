<SlideLayout title="Checking Schur's lemma — a worked example">

Let $V$ be a two-dimensional irrep of some algebra $A$. By the theorem:

$$\Homrep(V^{\oplus 2}, V^{\oplus 3}) \cong \Mat_{3 \times 2}(k).$$

So the space of intertwining operators $V^{\oplus 2} \to V^{\oplus 3}$ has dimension $3 \times 2 = 6$.
