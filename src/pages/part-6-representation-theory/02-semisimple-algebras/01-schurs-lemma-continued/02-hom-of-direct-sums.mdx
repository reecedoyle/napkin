<SlideLayout title="Hom-spaces of direct sums">

Let $V$ be a finite-dimensional irrep. What is $\Homrep(V^{\oplus m}, V^{\oplus n})$?

An intertwining operator $T \colon V^{\oplus m} \to V^{\oplus n}$ is determined by the $mn$ compositions

$$V \hookrightarrow V^{\oplus m} \xrightarrow{T} V^{\oplus n} \twoheadrightarrow V$$

where the first map includes into the $i$th copy and the last projects onto the $j$th copy. By Schur's lemma each composition is a scalar $c_{ij} \in k$.

So $T$ is given by an $n \times m$ matrix of scalars acting on $V$-valued column vectors:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
c_{11} & \cdots & c_{1m} \\
\vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
c_{n1} & \cdots & c_{nm}
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} v_1 \\ \vdots \\ v_m \end{bmatrix} \in V^{\oplus n}.
$$

<Callout kind="remark" title="Type mismatch">
The $c_{ij}$ are scalars in $k$ but the $v_i$ are vectors in $V$. This is not an ordinary matrix multiplication in $k^n$; the outputs are linear combinations of elements of $V$. The non-isomorphic irreps "do not talk to each other."
</Callout>

</SlideLayout>
