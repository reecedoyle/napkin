<SlideLayout title="Decomposing the permutation representation of S₃">

The natural **permutation representation** of $S_3$ acts on $V = \CC^{\oplus 3}$ by permuting the standard basis vectors $e_1, e_2, e_3$.

This is a three-dimensional representation. By the classification, it decomposes as

$$V = \refl_0 \oplus \CC_{\mathrm{triv}}.$$

Indeed: the trivial subrepresentation is spanned by $e_1 + e_2 + e_3$ (every permutation fixes this vector). The complement $\refl_0 = \{x + y + z = 0\}$ is the two-dimensional irrep.

<Callout kind="remark" title="No sign representation here">
The sign representation does not appear in the permutation representation of $S_3$ on $\CC^3$. The only summands are $\refl_0$ and $\CC_{\mathrm{triv}}$.
</Callout>
