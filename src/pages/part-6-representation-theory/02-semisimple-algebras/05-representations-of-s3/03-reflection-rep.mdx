<SlideLayout title="The reflection representation of S₃">

The two-dimensional irrep is the <Term k="reflectionRep">reflection representation</Term>.

<Callout kind="definition" title="Reflection representation refl₀">
Let $\refl_0$ be the representation of $S_3$ whose vector space is

$$\{ (x, y, z) \in \CC^3 \mid x + y + z = 0 \},$$

with $S_3$ acting by permuting coordinates: $\sigma \cdot (x,y,z) = (x_{\sigma^{-1}(1)}, x_{\sigma^{-1}(2)}, x_{\sigma^{-1}(3)})$.
</Callout>

This space has dimension 2 (the hyperplane $x + y + z = 0$ in $\CC^3$). A basis is $e_1 - e_2$ and $e_2 - e_3$.

The representation $\refl_0$ is irreducible: no one-dimensional subspace is invariant under all of $S_3$ (one can check this directly by showing any nonzero vector generates the whole space).

Since $\CC[S_3]$ is semisimple with three irreps of dimensions 1, 1, 2, we have

$$\CC[S_3] \cong \CC \oplus \CC \oplus \Mat_2(\CC).$$

</SlideLayout>
