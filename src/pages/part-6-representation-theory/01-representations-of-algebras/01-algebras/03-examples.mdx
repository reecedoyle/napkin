<SlideLayout title="Examples of k-algebras">

Let $k$ be any field. The following are all <Term k="kAlgebra">$k$-algebras</Term>:

- The field $k$ itself.
- The <Term k="polyRing">polynomial ring</Term> $k[x_1, \dots, x_n]$.
- The set <Term k="MatnK">$\Mat_n(k)$</Term> of $n \times n$ matrices with entries in $k$ (multiplication is noncommutative for $n \geq 2$).
- The set $\Mat(V)$ of linear maps $T \colon V \to V$ for a $k$-vector space $V$ — this is essentially $\Mat_n(k)$ once you pick a basis.
