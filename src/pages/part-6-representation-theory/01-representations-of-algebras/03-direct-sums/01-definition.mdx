<Callout kind="definition" title="Direct sum of representations">
Let $V = (V, \rho_V)$ and $W = (W, \rho_W)$ be representations of a $k$-algebra $A$. The <Term k="algDirectSum">**direct sum**</Term> $V \oplus W$ is the vector space $V \oplus W$ with action $a \cdot (v, w) = (a \cdot v, a \cdot w)$.
</Callout>

In matrix form, the action $\rho$ of $V \oplus W$ looks like a block-diagonal matrix:

$$\rho(a) = \begin{bmatrix} \rho_V(a) & 0 \\ 0 & \rho_W(a) \end{bmatrix}.$$

This is exactly what we saw with $\Mat_2(\mathbb{R})$ acting on $\mathbb{R}^{\oplus 4}$: each matrix acts on the two $\mathbb{R}^{\oplus 2}$ summands independently.
