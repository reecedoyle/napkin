<Problem
  difficulty="daggered"
  id="chr-prob-decomposition"
  prompt="Let $W$ be a complex representation of a finite group $G$. Let $V_1, \dots, V_r$ be the complex irreps of $G$ and set $n_i = \langle \chi_W, \chi_{V_i} \rangle$. Prove that each $n_i$ is a non-negative integer and $W = \bigoplus_{i=1}^r V_i^{\oplus n_i}$."
  hint="Write $W = \bigoplus_i V_i^{m_i}$ using semisimplicity of $\mathbb{C}[G]$. Then $\chi_W = \sum_i m_i \chi_{V_i}$, so $n_i = \langle \chi_W, \chi_{V_i} \rangle = m_i$ by orthonormality."
  solution="Since $\CC[G]$ is semisimple, we may write $W \cong \bigoplus_i V_i^{m_i}$ for non-negative integers $m_i$. Then $\chi_W = \sum_i m_i \chi_{V_i}$. By orthonormality of irreducible characters, $\langle \chi_W, \chi_{V_j} \rangle = m_j$. So $n_j = m_j \ge 0$ is a non-negative integer, and $W = \bigoplus_i V_i^{n_i}$."

<Problem
  difficulty="standard"
  id="chr-prob-refl0-tensor"
  prompt="Consider complex representations of $G = S_4$. The representation $\refl_0 \otimes \refl_0$ is $9$-dimensional, hence reducible. Compute its decomposition in terms of the five irreducible representations."
  hint="Use the previous problem with $\chi_W = \chi_{\refl_0}^2$, combined with the S₄ character table."
  solution="We have $\chi_{\refl_0}^2$ at each conjugacy class: $(9, 1, 0, 1, 1)$. Computing inner products with each irrep character and dividing by $|S_4| = 24$ gives $n_{\Csign} = 1$, $n_{\CC^2} = 1$, $n_{\refl_0} = 1$, $n_{\refl_0 \otimes \Csign} = 1$, $n_{\Ctriv} = 0$. So $\refl_0 \otimes \refl_0 \cong \Csign \oplus \CC^2 \oplus \refl_0 \oplus (\refl_0 \otimes \Csign)$."
/>

<Problem
  difficulty="standard"
  id="chr-prob-tensoring-1d"
  prompt="Let $V$ and $W$ be irreps of $G$ with $\dim W = 1$. Show that $V \otimes W$ is irreducible."
  hint="Note that $|\chi_W(g)| = 1$ for all $g \in G$, since $W$ is one-dimensional and all eigenvalues are roots of unity."
  solution="Since $\dim W = 1$, the action of each $g$ is multiplication by a scalar $\chi_W(g)$, which is a root of unity, so $|\chi_W(g)| = 1$. Then $\langle \chi_{V \otimes W}, \chi_{V \otimes W} \rangle = \frac{1}{|G|} \sum_g |\chi_V(g)|^2 |\chi_W(g)|^2 = \frac{1}{|G|} \sum_g |\chi_V(g)|^2 = \langle \chi_V, \chi_V \rangle = 1$. So $V \otimes W$ is irreducible."
/>
