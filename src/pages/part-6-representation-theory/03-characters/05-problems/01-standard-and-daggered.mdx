  prompt="Let W be a complex representation of a finite group G. Let V₁, …, Vᵣ be the complex irreps of G and set nᵢ = ⟨χ_W, χ_{Vᵢ}⟩. Prove that each nᵢ is a non-negative integer and W = ⊕ᵢ Vᵢ^{⊕nᵢ}."
  hint="Write W = ⊕ Vᵢ^{mᵢ} using semisimplicity of ℂ[G]. Then χ_W = Σ mᵢ χ_{Vᵢ}, so nᵢ = ⟨χ_W, χ_{Vᵢ}⟩ = mᵢ by orthonormality."
  solution="Since ℂ[G] is semisimple, we may write W ≅ ⊕ Vᵢ^{mᵢ} for non-negative integers mᵢ. Then χ_W = Σ mᵢ χ_{Vᵢ}. By orthonormality of irreducible characters, ⟨χ_W, χ_{Vⱼ}⟩ = mⱼ. So nⱼ = mⱼ ≥ 0 is a non-negative integer, and W = ⊕ Vᵢ^{nᵢ}."

<Problem
  client:load
  difficulty="standard"
  id="chr-prob-tensoring-1d"
  prompt="Let V and W be irreps of G with dim W = 1. Show that V ⊗ W is irreducible."
  hint="Note that |χ_W(g)| = 1 for all g ∈ G, since W is one-dimensional and group elements have finite order so all eigenvalues are roots of unity."
  solution="Since dim W = 1, each g acts on W by multiplication by the scalar χ_W(g), a root of unity, so |χ_W(g)| = 1. Then ⟨χ_{V⊗W}, χ_{V⊗W}⟩ = (1/|G|) Σ_g |χ_V(g)|² |χ_W(g)|² = (1/|G|) Σ_g |χ_V(g)|² = ⟨χ_V, χ_V⟩ = 1. So V ⊗ W is irreducible."
