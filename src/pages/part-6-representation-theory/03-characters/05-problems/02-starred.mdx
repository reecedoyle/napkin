<Problem
  client:load
  difficulty="starred"
  id="chr-prob-second-orthog"
  prompt="Let g, h be elements of a finite group G and let V₁, …, Vᵣ be the irreps of G. Prove the second orthogonality formula: Σᵢ χ_{Vᵢ}(g) · conjugate(χ_{Vᵢ}(h)) = |C_G(g)| if g and h are conjugate, and 0 otherwise. Here C_G(g) = {x ∈ G : xg = gx}."
  hint="Construct two square r × r matrices A and B such that AB = I using the first orthogonality. Then since AB = I implies BA = I for square matrices, use BA to derive the second orthogonality formula."
  solution="Index rows by irreps and columns by conjugacy classes. Let A_{ij} = χ_{Vᵢ}(Cⱼ) √(|Cⱼ|/|G|) and B = A* (conjugate transpose). The first orthogonality relation says (AA*)_{ij} = δᵢⱼ, i.e. AA* = I. Since A is a square r × r matrix, AA* = I implies A*A = I. The (j,k) entry of A*A = I gives Σᵢ A*_{ji} A_{ik} = Σᵢ (|Cⱼ|/|G|)^{1/2} χ̄_{Vᵢ}(Cⱼ) χ_{Vᵢ}(Cₖ) (|Cₖ|/|G|)^{1/2} = δⱼₖ. Multiplying through by |G|/(|Cⱼ||Cₖ|)^{1/2} and then evaluating at j = k gives the second orthogonality formula. Since |Cⱼ| = |G|/|C_G(gⱼ)| by orbit-stabilizer, the diagonal entry becomes |C_G(g)|."
/>
