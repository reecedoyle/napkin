<SlideLayout title="An inner product on class functions">

From here we specialize to finite groups $G$ represented over $\CC$.
The character table's "dot product" rows (Property IV) is an actual inner product.

<Callout kind="definition" title="Inner product on class functions">
Let <Term k="classFunctions">$\FunCl(G)$</Term> be the $\CC$-vector space of functions $\Classes(G) \to \CC$.
We equip it with the **Hermitian inner product**
$$\langle f_1, f_2 \rangle = \frac{1}{|G|} \sum_{g \in G} f_1(g)\, \ol{f_2(g)}.$$
</Callout>

This is exactly the "dot product" from the $S_3$ table:

- $\langle \chi_{\Ctriv}, \chi_{\Csign} \rangle = \frac{1}{6}(1 \cdot 1 + 3 \cdot (1)(-1) + 2 \cdot (1)(1)) = \frac{1+(-3)+2}{6} = 0$.

The **orthogonality theorem** says the irreducible characters form an orthonormal basis of $\FunCl(G)$.
This simultaneously proves Properties (III) and (IV).
