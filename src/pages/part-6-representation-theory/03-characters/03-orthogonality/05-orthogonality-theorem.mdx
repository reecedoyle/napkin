<SlideLayout title="The orthogonality theorem">

We can now state and prove the main theorem of this chapter.

<Callout kind="theorem" title="Orthogonality">
For any finite-dimensional complex representations $V$ and $W$ of $G$:
$$\langle \chi_V, \chi_W \rangle = \dim \Homrep(W, V).$$
In particular, for irreps:
$$\langle \chi_V, \chi_W \rangle = \begin{cases} 1 & V \cong W \\ 0 & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$$
</Callout>

<Callout kind="corollary" title="Irreps give an orthonormal basis">
The characters of all irreducible representations of $G$ form an **orthonormal basis** of <Term k="classFunctions">$\FunCl(G)$</Term>.
</Callout>

This simultaneously proves Properties (III) (norm of each irrep character is 1, giving $\sum_i d_i^2 = |G|$) and Property (IV) (distinct irrep characters are orthogonal).
