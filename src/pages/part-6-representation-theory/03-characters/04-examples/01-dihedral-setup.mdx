We compute the character table of the dihedral group $D_{10} = \langle r, s \mid r^5 = s^2 = 1,\; rs = sr^{-1} \rangle$ (order $10$).

Let $\omega = \exp(2\pi i / 5)$.
The group has **four conjugacy classes**: $\{1\}$, $\{r, r^4\}$, $\{r^2, r^3\}$, and $\{sr^k : k = 0,\dots, 4\}$.
So there are exactly four irreps.

<Callout kind="example" title="Four representations of D₁₀">
- $\Ctriv$: all elements act as the identity.
- $\Csign$: $r \mapsto 1$, $s \mapsto -1$.
- $V_1$ (2-dimensional): $r \mapsto \begin{bmatrix} \omega & 0 \\ 0 & \omega^4 \end{bmatrix}$, $s \mapsto \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$.
- $V_2$ (2-dimensional): $r \mapsto \begin{bmatrix} \omega^2 & 0 \\ 0 & \omega^3 \end{bmatrix}$, $s \mapsto \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$.
</Callout>

The dimensions satisfy $1^2 + 1^2 + 2^2 + 2^2 = 10 = |D_{10}|$, consistent with Property (III).
