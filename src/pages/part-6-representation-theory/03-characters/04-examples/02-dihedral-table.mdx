Reading off the traces from the matrix definitions gives:

<Callout kind="example" title="D₁₀ character table">
| $D_{10}$ | $1$ | $r, r^4$ | $r^2, r^3$ | $sr^k$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $\Ctriv$ | $1$ | $1$ | $1$ | $1$ |
| $\Csign$ | $1$ | $1$ | $1$ | $-1$ |
| $V_1$ | $2$ | $\omega + \omega^4$ | $\omega^2 + \omega^3$ | $0$ |
| $V_2$ | $2$ | $\omega^2 + \omega^3$ | $\omega + \omega^4$ | $0$ |

The columns represent $1 + 2 + 2 + 5 = 10$ elements in total.
</Callout>

Note: $\omega + \omega^4 = 2\cos(2\pi/5) \approx 0.618$ and $\omega^2 + \omega^3 = 2\cos(4\pi/5) \approx -1.618$.

**Verifying orthogonality:** $\langle \Ctriv, \Csign \rangle = \frac{1}{10}(1 + 2 \cdot 1 + 2 \cdot 1 + 5 \cdot (-1)) = \frac{1+2+2-5}{10} = 0$. ✓

A direct norm computation shows each row has norm $1$, confirming all four representations are irreducible and pairwise non-isomorphic.
