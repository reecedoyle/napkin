<Callout kind="example" title="Power map on ℂ∞">
The map $z \mapsto z^k$, extended to a map $\CC_\infty \to \CC_\infty$ (sending $\infty \mapsto \infty$), has <Term k="rs48degreeMap">degree</Term> $k$.

For a nonzero $t \neq 0$, the equation $z^k = t$ has exactly $k$ distinct solutions — the $k$-th roots of $t$, each with multiplicity 1. So the total multiplicity of the fiber above $t$ is $k$.

For $t = 0$, the preimage is just $\{0\}$, but $0$ is mapped with multiplicity $k$ — the map is exactly $z^k$, which vanishes to order $k$ at the origin (the order of vanishing is just the exponent). So the total multiplicity is still $k$.
</Callout>

The theme: counted with multiplicity, holomorphic maps are very regular. We will make the notion of multiplicity precise in the next section.
