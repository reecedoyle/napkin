<SlideLayout title="Compact Riemann surfaces and degree">

The Riemann sphere $\CC_\infty$ is compact — it is a closed, bounded surface. Compact Riemann surfaces enjoy especially nice properties.

Here is a striking one: for a holomorphic map between compact Riemann surfaces, every point in the target has exactly the same number of preimages, counted with multiplicity.

<Callout kind="proposition" title="Constant fiber size">
Let $X$ and $Y$ be compact Riemann surfaces, and let $f \colon X \to Y$ be holomorphic and not constant. For each point $y \in Y$, let $d_y$ be the total multiplicity of points in the preimage $f^{-1}(y)$.

Then $d_y$ is well-defined and constant — it takes the same value for every $y \in Y$.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="definition" title="Degree of a map">
The constant value $d_y$ above is called the <Term k="rs48degreeMap">degree</Term> of the map $f$, written $\deg(f)$.
</Callout>
