<SlideLayout title="The correspondence between meromorphic and holomorphic">

The previous slide's construction goes both ways.

<Callout kind="proposition" title="Meromorphic ↔ holomorphic into ℂ∞">
There is a one-to-one correspondence between <Term k="rs48meromorphicFunction">meromorphic functions</Term> $f \colon X \to \CC$ and holomorphic maps $g \colon X \to \CC_\infty$ such that $g$ is not identically $\infty$.
</Callout>

The moral is worth remembering:

<Callout kind="remark" title="The key idea">
Plugging in the hole at $\infty$ of $\CC$ allows us to analytically extend meromorphic functions to maps $X \to \CC \cup \{\infty\}$ that are holomorphic everywhere — no singularities, no special cases.
</Callout>

This reframes meromorphic functions as a special case of morphisms between Riemann surfaces, bringing them under the same framework we've just set up.
