The sum-of-orders proposition is an instance of a recurring theme in complex analysis and Riemann surface theory.

<Callout kind="remark" title="Recurring theme">
If the "things" (zeros, poles, preimages) are counted properly — with multiplicity — the formula is very nice. The total count is always the same, regardless of the point chosen.
</Callout>

This is fundamentally a consequence of holomorphicity: the rigidity of holomorphic maps forces these counts to be globally constant in ways that smooth or continuous maps need not be.
