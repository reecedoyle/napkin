<Callout kind="definition" title="Degree of a divisor">
For a divisor $D = \sum_p n_p \cdot p$ on a compact Riemann surface $X$, its <Term k="rs51degDiv">degree</Term> is

$$
\deg D = \sum_{p \in X} D(p) = \sum_p n_p.
$$
</Callout>

The sum is finite because $D$ has finite support. Degree is a group homomorphism $\Div(X) \to \ZZ$: it is additive, $\deg(D_1 + D_2) = \deg D_1 + \deg D_2$.

<Callout kind="example" title="Prototype">
$$
\deg\bigl((-3) \cdot i + (-4) \cdot \infty\bigr) = -3 + (-4) = -7.
$$
</Callout>
