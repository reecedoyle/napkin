<Callout kind="definition" title="Line bundle">
A **line bundle** $L$ over a Riemann surface $X$ is a set together with:

1. A <Term k="rs52projectionMap">projection map</Term> $\pi \colon L \to X$.
2. An open cover $\{U_i\}$ of $X$.
3. For each $U_i$, a <Term k="rs52lineBundleChart">line bundle chart</Term> $\phi \colon \pi^{-1}(U_i) \to \mathbb{C} \times U_i$ that restricts to a bijection $\pi^{-1}(p) \to \mathbb{C} \times \{p\}$ for each $p \in U_i$.
4. For any two open sets $U_1, U_2$, the <Term k="rs52transitionFunction">transition function</Term>
$$\phi_2 \circ \phi_1^{-1} \colon \mathbb{C} \times (U_1 \cap U_2) \to \mathbb{C} \times (U_1 \cap U_2)$$
must be a $\mathbb{C}$-vector space isomorphism on each fiber $\mathbb{C} \times \{p\}$, with an analytic scaling factor.
</Callout>

The dense definition packages two compatible structures: an **analytic** one (charts are holomorphic) and a **$1$-dimensional vector space** one (fibers are copies of $\mathbb{C}$). The transition functions are exactly the glue that welds the local pieces into a coherent global object.
