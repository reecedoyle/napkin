<Callout kind="remark" title="Prototype">
The Riemann sphere and connected open subsets of $\CC$ are the two most important prototypes. The sphere is the unique compact Riemann surface of genus $0$. Open subsets of $\CC$ are the basic non-compact examples that require no welding.
