<Callout kind="remark" title="Kernels, generalized">
Whenever a category has a distinguished "trivial map" between any two objects (as $\mathrm{Grp}$ does — the map sending everything to the identity), equalizers give us a categorical notion of **kernel**: the kernel of $\phi \colon G \to H$ is just the equalizer of $\phi$ and the trivial homomorphism $G \to H$. We'll flesh this idea out properly in the chapter on abelian categories.
</Callout>
