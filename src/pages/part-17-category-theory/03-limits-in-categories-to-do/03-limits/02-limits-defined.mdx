<SlideLayout title="The limit is the universal cone">

Now do to a general diagram exactly what we did to products and to forks: demand the cone be universal.

<Callout kind="definition" title="Limit">
A <Term k="lim68Limit">**limit**</Term> of a diagram $D$ is a universal cone over $D$: a cone through which every other cone over $D$ factors, by a unique map.
</Callout>

This is about as general a definition as you can ask for — products and equalizers are both limits, just over different shapes of diagram. And as always with anything defined by a universal property: when a limit of $D$ exists, it's unique up to a unique isomorphism.
