<Callout kind="example" title="More exact sequences">
The sequence $0 \to \ZZ \taking{\times 3} \ZZ \to \Zc3 \to 0$ is short exact. So is $0 \to \ZZ \taking{\times 5} \ZZ \to \Zc5 \to 0$.
</Callout>

As these examples show, the slogan "$C \cong B/A$" from the last slide isn't the *whole* story — the map $A \to B$ matters in determining what $C$ actually is. This is exactly the common notation at work: the image of $\ZZ \taking{\times 3} \ZZ$ is usually written $3\ZZ$, so $\Zc3 = \ZZ / 3\ZZ$.
