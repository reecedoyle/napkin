The integral cellular chain complex has each Cₖ ≅ ℤ, and the boundary maps alternate between multiplication by 0 and by 2: ∂ₖ is multiplication by 1 + (−1)ᵏ, i.e. it is ×2 when k is even and ×0 when k is odd. Now apply the cellular cochain functor Hom(−; ℤ/2ℤ). Each cochain group is Hom(ℤ, ℤ/2ℤ) ≅ ℤ/2ℤ, and every coboundary map is the dual of ×2 or ×0. But multiplication by 2 is the zero map on ℤ/2ℤ, and ×0 is of course zero. Hence every coboundary map in the mod-2 cellular cochain complex is zero. Therefore the cohomology equals the cochain groups themselves: Hᵐ(ℝℙⁿ; ℤ/2ℤ) ≅ ℤ/2ℤ for each 0 ≤ m ≤ n, and 0 in all other degrees.
