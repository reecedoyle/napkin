<Callout kind="question" title="Hⁿ as a functor">
Interpret <Term k="sc74HnAsFunctor">$H^n \colon \catname{CoCmplx} \to \catname{Grp}$</Term> as a functor: a cochain map $A^\bullet \to B^\bullet$ induces a homomorphism $H^n(A^\bullet) \to H^n(B^\bullet)$.
</Callout>

Compose the two functors from the previous slide with this one:

$$
\catname{Cmplx}\op \taking{\Hom(-,G)} \catname{CoCmplx} \taking{H^n} \catname{Grp}
$$

to get a single contravariant functor $H^n(-;G) \colon \catname{Cmplx}\op \to \catname{Grp}$.

Now stack $C_\bullet \colon \catname{Top} \to \catname{Cmplx}$ on the front, and we have assembled all the pieces needed to send a *space* (contravariantly) to a group.
