<SlideLayout title="Relative cohomology groups">

Just as with homology, we can also define **relative** cohomology in the obvious way: dualize the relative chain complex
$$
\dots \taking\partial C_1(X,A) \taking\partial C_0(X,A) \to 0
$$
to get a cochain complex
$$
\dots \xleftarrow\delta C^1(X,A;G) \xleftarrow\delta C^0(X,A;G) \leftarrow 0,
$$
and take its cohomology.

<Callout kind="definition" title="Relative cohomology groups">
The groups obtained this way are the <Term k="sc74RelativeCohomology">relative cohomology groups</Term>, denoted $H^n(X,A;G)$.
</Callout>
