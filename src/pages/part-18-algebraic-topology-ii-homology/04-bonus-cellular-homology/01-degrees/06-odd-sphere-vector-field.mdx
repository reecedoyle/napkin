<Callout kind="remark" title="Why even dimension is essential">
The hairy ball argument only forced a contradiction from $\deg(\id) = \deg(-\id)$ when $n$ is even. For odd $n$, $\deg(-\id) = (-1)^{n+1} = 1 = \deg(\id)$ already — no contradiction, and indeed nonvanishing fields exist. This is why the theorem is specifically about even-dimensional spheres: $S^1$, $S^3$, $S^5, \dots$ can all be combed flat, but $S^2$, $S^4, \dots$ cannot.
</Callout>
