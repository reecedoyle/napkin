Roughly speaking:

<Callout kind="remark" title="Moral">
$\deg f$ counts how many times $\img f$ wraps around $S^n$ — or how many "$S^n$ bags" worth of covering $\img f$ consists of.
</Callout>

A few worked examples:

- For $n = 1$, the map $z \mapsto z^k$ (viewing $S^1 \subseteq \CC$) has degree $k$.
- A reflection map $(x_0, x_1, \dots, x_n) \mapsto (-x_0, x_1, \dots, x_n)$ has degree $-1$ — geometrically this flips orientation once.
- The <Term k="bch73AntipodalMap">antipodal map</Term> $x \mapsto -x$ has degree $(-1)^{n+1}$, since it's a composition of $n+1$ reflections like the one above.
