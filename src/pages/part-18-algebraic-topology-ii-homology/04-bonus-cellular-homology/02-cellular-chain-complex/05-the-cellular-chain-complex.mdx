<SlideLayout title="The cellular chain complex">

Since consecutive composites vanish, we get an honest chain complex:
$$
\cdots \xrightarrow{d_5} \Cells_4(X) \xrightarrow{d_4} \Cells_3(X) \xrightarrow{d_3} \Cells_2(X) \xrightarrow{d_2} \Cells_1(X) \xrightarrow{d_1} \Cells_0(X) \xrightarrow{d_0} 0.
$$

<Callout kind="definition" title="Cellular chain complex">
This is called the <Term k="bch73CellularChainComplex">cellular chain complex</Term> of $X$.
</Callout>

Every term is free abelian (basis: the cells), and for any reasonable CW complex it's also finitely generated — a world apart from the unwieldy singular chain complex $C_\bullet(X)$. In other words, $\Cells_\bullet(X)$ is a small, concrete model built entirely out of counting cells and computing a handful of degree maps.

</SlideLayout>
