<SlideLayout title="Cellular homology equals singular homology">

Here is the payoff for building all this machinery:

<Callout kind="theorem" title="Cellular chain complex gives H_n(X)">
The $k$-th homology group of the <Term k="bch73CellularChainComplex">cellular chain complex</Term> is isomorphic to $H_k(X)$.
</Callout>

This is why we bothered: instead of computing $H_k(X)$ from the enormous singular chain complex, we can compute the homology of $\Cells_\bullet(X)$ — a complex with one generator per cell — and get the same answer.

The proof is a genuine diagram chase through the interlocking exact sequences from the previous slides; we leave it as a problem at the end of this chapter, where you'll show it concretely for $H_2(X^2, X^1)$.
