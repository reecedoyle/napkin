For a single finitely generated abelian group G_n, choose a presentation G_n ≅ ℤ^r ⊕ (ℤ/d₁ℤ) ⊕ ⋯ ⊕ (ℤ/d_mℤ). Build X_n by starting with a wedge of r copies of Sⁿ (giving the free part ℤ^r in degree n as its cellular chains), then for each cyclic factor ℤ/d_iℤ attach an (n+1)-cell to a chosen n-sphere in the wedge via a degree-d_i self-map of Sⁿ. By the cellular boundary formula, this (n+1)-cell contributes a differential that is multiplication by d_i onto that sphere's ℤ summand and zero on the others, since the attaching map only touches one wedge factor. The resulting cellular chain complex is concentrated in degrees n+1 and n; computing ker/img gives H_n(X_n) ≅ ℤ^r ⊕ ℤ/d₁ℤ ⊕ ⋯ ⊕ ℤ/d_mℤ ≅ G_n exactly, with all other reduced homology groups of X_n vanishing since X_n has no other cells. Taking X = X₁ ∨ ⋯ ∨ X_N realizes every G_n simultaneously, each in its own degree."
