<SlideLayout title="What exactly is the fuzz?">

By "fuzz," we just mean: an aligned cycle can be nudged around a bit — reparametrized, or shifted by adding an element of $B_1(X^1, X^0)$ — while remaining a cycle in $Z_1(X)$. The same goes for aligned boundaries. Since the cellular chain complex quotients out exactly this wiggle-room on both the numerator and the denominator of "cycle over boundary," the common fuzz factor cancels and what's left over is the same as ordinary singular homology.
