<SlideLayout title="The cohomology ring of a wedge sum">

<Callout kind="theorem" title="Cohomology pseudo-rings of wedge sums">
$$
\wt H^\bullet(X \vee Y; R) \cong \wt H^\bullet(X;R) \times \wt H^\bullet(Y;R)
$$
as graded pseudo-rings.
</Callout>

Knowing the two sides are abstractly isomorphic is nice, but knowing *which* map realizes the isomorphism is much more useful — it's what lets you actually compute with specific examples, rather than just knowing existence.
