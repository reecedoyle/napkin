<SlideLayout title="The Künneth theorem">

<Callout kind="theorem" title="Künneth formula">
Let $X$ and $Y$ be CW complexes such that $H^k(Y;R)$ is a finitely generated free $R$-module for every $k$. Then the cross product is an isomorphism of anticommutative rings
$$
H^\bullet(X;R) \otimes_R H^\bullet(Y;R) \to H^\bullet(X\times Y;R).
$$
</Callout>

<Callout kind="remark" title="Moral">
There is a one-to-one correspondence between pairs of holes in $X$ and $Y$, and holes of $X\times Y$ — and the correspondence respects the cup product. Here "holes" means "generators of cohomology groups".
</Callout>

This finally makes precise the informal moral from the start of the section — a $m$-hole in $X$ and an $n$-hole in $Y$ really do combine into an $(m+n)$-hole in $X\times Y$, and moreover the *ring* structure (not just the raw groups) transfers over cleanly.

</SlideLayout>
