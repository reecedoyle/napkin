One more degenerate case worth internalizing before we move on: what happens when $A = X$?

<Callout kind="remark">
Take $A = X$ in the contractible-subspace lemma's proof pattern. Since $C_n(X,X) = C_n(X)/C_n(X) = 0$ for every $n$, the relative chain complex is entirely zero, so its homology vanishes identically — no long exact sequence needed at all.
</Callout>
