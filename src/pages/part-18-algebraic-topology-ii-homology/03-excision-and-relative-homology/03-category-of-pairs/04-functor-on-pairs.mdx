<SlideLayout title="Homology as a functor on pairs">

Running the same prism-operator argument used for ordinary homotopy invariance (just applied one dimension up, to pairs) gives:

<Callout kind="lemma" title="Induced maps of relative homology">
$H_n$ is a <Term k="ear72Functor">functor</Term>
$$
H_n \colon \catname{hPairTop} \to \catname{Grp}.
$$
</Callout>

Spelled out: a map of pairs $f \colon (X,A) \to (Y,B)$ induces a group homomorphism
$$
f_\ast \colon H_n(X,A) \to H_n(Y,B),
$$
and if $f, g \colon (X,A) \to (Y,B)$ are pair-homotopic, then $f_\ast = g_\ast$ — pair-homotopic maps induce identical maps on relative homology. This is exactly what lets us replace a geometric argument (deforming a map of pairs) with an algebraic one (comparing induced homomorphisms).

</SlideLayout>
