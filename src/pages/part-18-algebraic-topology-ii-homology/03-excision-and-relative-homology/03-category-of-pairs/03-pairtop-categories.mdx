<SlideLayout title="PairTop and hPairTop">

This gives us two <Term k="ear72Category">categories</Term>, mirroring $\catname{Top}$ and $\catname{hTop}$ for ordinary spaces:

- $\catname{PairTop}$, the category of pairs of topological spaces, with maps of pairs as morphisms.
- $\catname{hPairTop}$, the same category but with morphisms identified whenever they're pair-homotopic.

<Callout kind="definition" title="Pair-homotopy equivalence">
Pairs $(X,A)$ and $(Y,B)$ are <Term k="ear72PairHomotopyEquivalence">pair-homotopy equivalent</Term> if they are isomorphic in $\catname{hPairTop}$. An isomorphism of $\catname{hPairTop}$ is called a pair-homotopy equivalence.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="remark">
Pair-homotopy equivalence is the natural generalization of ordinary homotopy equivalence of spaces: taking $A = B = \varnothing$, a map of pairs is just an ordinary map $X \to Y$, and $X$ is homotopy equivalent to $Y$ exactly when $(X,\varnothing)$ is pair-homotopy equivalent to $(Y,\varnothing)$.
</Callout>
