Here's a useful consequence of combining deformation retracts with the long exact sequence of a triple.

<Callout kind="exercise" title="Replacing A with a deformation-retract neighborhood">
Show that if $A \subseteq V \subseteq X$ and $A$ is a deformation retract of $V$, then $H_n(X,A) \cong H_n(X,V)$ for all $n$.
</Callout>

Since A is a deformation retract of V, we already showed H_k(V,A) = 0 for every k. Plugging in zero on both sides of H_n(X,A) → H_n(X,V) forces this map to be both injective and surjective by exactness — so it's an isomorphism, giving H_n(X,A) ≅ H_n(X,V)."
