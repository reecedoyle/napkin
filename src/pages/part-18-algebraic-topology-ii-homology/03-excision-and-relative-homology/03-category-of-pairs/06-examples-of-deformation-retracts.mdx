<Callout kind="example" title="Examples of deformation retracts">
(a) If a single point $p$ is a deformation retract of $X$, then $X$ is <Term k="ear72Contractible">contractible</Term>: the retraction $r \colon X \to \{p\}$, viewed as a map $X \to X$, is homotopic to $\id_X$.

(b) The punctured disk $D^2 \setminus \{0\}$ deformation retracts onto its boundary $S^1$.

(c) More generally, $D^n \setminus \{0\}$ deformation retracts onto its boundary $S^{n-1}$.

(d) Similarly, $\RR^n \setminus \{0\}$ deformation retracts onto a sphere $S^{n-1}$.
</Callout>
