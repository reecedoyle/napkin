<SlideLayout title="Sphere homology by induction">

The pattern from $(D^2,S^1)$ generalizes: the pair $(D^m, S^{m-1})$ gives, by the identical argument,
$$
\wt{H}_n(S^m) \cong \wt{H}_{n-1}(S^{m-1})
$$
for any $n, m > 1$. Combined with two base cases we already know from earlier chapters —

- $\wt{H}_0(S^n)$ is $\ZZ$ when $n = 0$ and $0$ otherwise,
- $\wt{H}_n(S^0)$ is $\ZZ$ when $n = 0$ and $0$ otherwise,

— induction on $\min\{m,n\}$ pins down every reduced homology group of every sphere:
$$
\wt{H}_n(S^m) \cong
\begin{cases}
\ZZ & m = n \\
0 & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
$$

This matches what Mayer–Vietoris gave us in an earlier chapter — a good consistency check — but the excision route generalizes more readily to other spaces built from spheres and disks.
