<Callout kind="remark" title="Proof">
Apply the good pair theorem to the disjoint union $X \amalg Y$ with the two-point subset $\{x_0, y_0\}$:
$$
\wt{H}_n(X \vee Y)
\cong \wt{H}_n\big( (X \amalg Y)/\{x_0,y_0\} \big)
\cong H_n(X \amalg Y, \{x_0,y_0\}).
$$
Since $X \amalg Y$ is a disjoint union, its relative homology splits along the two components:
$$
H_n(X \amalg Y, \{x_0,y_0\}) \cong H_n(X,\{x_0\}) \oplus H_n(Y,\{y_0\}) \cong \wt{H}_n(X) \oplus \wt{H}_n(Y),
$$
using "relative homology generalizes absolute homology" from §2 in the last step.
</Callout>

Each arrow in this chain is one of the tools we've already built: the good pair theorem, disjoint-union additivity of relative homology, and the point-relative lemma.
