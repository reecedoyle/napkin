  id="sh70-prob-no-retraction"
  difficulty="standard"
  prompt="For n ≥ 1, show that the composition S^(n−1) ↪ D^n →F→ S^(n−1) cannot be the identity map on S^(n−1), for any continuous F. (Here D^n is the closed n-disk, S^(n−1) is its boundary sphere, and the first map is the inclusion.)"
  hint="Take the (n−1)st homology groups of the composition."
  solution="Apply the functor H_(n−1) to the whole composition S^(n−1) ↪ D^n →F→ S^(n−1). Since D^n is contractible, H_(n−1)(D^n) = 0 for n ≥ 1 (using n−1 ≥ 0). So on homology the composition becomes ℤ → 0 → ℤ (using H_(n−1)(S^(n−1)) ≅ ℤ at both ends), induced by H_(n−1) applied to the two maps. If the original composition were the identity on S^(n−1), then by functoriality the induced composition ℤ → 0 → ℤ would have to be the identity on ℤ — but any map that factors through the trivial group 0 is the zero map, and the zero map on ℤ is not the identity. Contradiction, so no such continuous F exists."
