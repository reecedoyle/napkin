Computing directly, $\partial c = ([v_1]-[v_0]) - ([v_2]-[v_0]) + ([v_2]-[v_1]) = 0$, so $c$ has trivial boundary — it's a "cycle". But $c$ is *not* itself the boundary of any $2$-chain: because of the hole in the middle, there's no way to "fill in" the interior of $c$ with a disk-shaped $2$-chain in $X$.

So we've detected the hole by a purely algebraic fact:
$$
c \in \ker\left( C_1(X) \taking{\partial} C_0(X) \right)
\qquad\text{but}\qquad
c \notin \img \left( C_2(X) \taking{\partial} C_1(X) \right).
$$
If the hole weren't there, $c$ *would* bound a disk, and this statement would be false. This kernel-vs-image gap is exactly what we're about to name and generalize.
