<Callout kind="definition" title="Reduced homology groups">
The homology groups of the augmented chain complex are the <Term k="sh70ReducedHomology">reduced homology groups</Term> $\wt H_n(X)$ of $X$.

Since the augmented complex only changes things at the very end, $\wt H_n(X) \cong H_n(X)$ for $n > 0$. At $n = 0$: the map $H_0(X) \to \ZZ$ induced by $\eps$ has kernel $\wt H_0(X)$, so $H_0(X) \cong \wt H_0(X) \oplus \ZZ$.
</Callout>

This is mostly a convenience: for instance, since a contractible space has $H_0 \cong \ZZ$ and every other $H_n$ trivial, its reduced homology groups $\wt H_n$ *all* vanish — no special-casing $n=0$ ever again.
