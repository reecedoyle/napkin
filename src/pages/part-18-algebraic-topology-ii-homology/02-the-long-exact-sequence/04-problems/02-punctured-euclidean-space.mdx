For p = 1: ℝ^n minus a point deformation retracts onto a sphere S^{n-1} around it, so this is exactly the sphere computation with m = n-1, giving H̃_{n-1} ≅ ℤ and everything else 0. For general p, use Mayer-Vietoris directly: let U be ℝ^n with all p points removed, and let V be the union of p small disjoint open balls, one centered at each removed point. Then U ∪ V = ℝ^n, which is contractible, so all reduced homology groups of U ∪ V vanish. The intersection U ∩ V is p disjoint punctured balls, each homotopy equivalent to S^{n-1}, so H̃_k(U ∩ V) ≅ ℤ^⊕p when k = n-1 and 0 otherwise. Reading off the Mayer-Vietoris sequence with H̃_k(V) = 0 (each ball is contractible) and H̃_k(U ∪ V) = 0 on both sides of H̃_k(U ∩ V), the connecting map gives H̃_k(U) ≅ H̃_{k-1}(U ∩ V) shifted appropriately, landing on H̃_{n-1}(U) ≅ ℤ^⊕p and 0 in every other degree."
