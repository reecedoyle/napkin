<SlideLayout title="The Mayer–Vietoris sequence, provisionally">

<Callout kind="remark" title="Moral">
Short exact sequences of chain complexes give long exact sequences of homology groups.
</Callout>

Let's cash this in on our four examples from the last section. Feeding the <Term k="tle71MVShortExact">Mayer–Vietoris short exact sequence</Term> into the long exact sequence theorem gives, for $X = U \cup V$ an open cover:

<Callout kind="example" title="Mayer–Vietoris long exact sequence, provisional version">
$$
\dots \to H_n(U \cap V) \to H_n(U) \oplus H_n(V) \to H_n(U+V) \to H_{n-1}(U \cap V) \to \dots
$$
and its reduced version
$$
\dots \to \wt H_n(U \cap V) \to \wt H_n(U) \oplus \wt H_n(V) \to \wt H_n(U+V) \to \wt H_{n-1}(U \cap V) \to \dots
$$
</Callout>

We call this version "provisional" because $H_n(U+V)$ — chains subordinate to the cover — is an awkward group to think about directly. In the next section we'll show it's secretly just $H_n(X)$, which turns this into the sequence you actually want to use.

</SlideLayout>
