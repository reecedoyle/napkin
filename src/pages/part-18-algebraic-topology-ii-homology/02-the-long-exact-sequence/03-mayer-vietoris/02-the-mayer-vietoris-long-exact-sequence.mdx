<SlideLayout title="The Mayer–Vietoris long exact sequence">

Combine the open cover theorem with the provisional Mayer–Vietoris sequence from the last section — every $H_n(U+V)$ and $\wt H_n(U+V)$ can now be rewritten as $H_n(X)$ and $\wt H_n(X)$:

<Callout kind="theorem" title="Mayer–Vietoris long exact sequence">
If $X = U \cup V$ is an open cover, then there are <Term k="tle71MVLES">long exact sequences</Term>
$$
\dots \to H_n(U \cap V) \to H_n(U) \oplus H_n(V) \to H_n(X) \to H_{n-1}(U \cap V) \to \dots
$$
and
$$
\dots \to \wt H_n(U \cap V) \to \wt H_n(U) \oplus \wt H_n(V) \to \wt H_n(X) \to \wt H_{n-1}(U \cap V) \to \dots
$$
</Callout>

This is the sequence you'll actually use in practice: it directly relates the homology of $X$ to the homology of two (hopefully simpler) open pieces $U, V$, and their intersection. Everything from here on is applying it to specific spaces.

</SlideLayout>
