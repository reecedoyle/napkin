Slide the same segment of the sequence one degree down:
$$
\dots \taking{\phi} \underbrace{\wt H_1(U) \oplus \wt H_1(V)}_{\cong \ZZ \oplus \ZZ} \taking{\psi} \wt H_1(X) \taking{\partial} \underbrace{\wt H_0(U \cap V)}_{\cong \ZZ} \to \underbrace{\wt H_0(U) \oplus \wt H_0(V)}_{=0} \to \dots
$$

The right-hand zero forces $\partial$ to be surjective, so $\img\,\partial \cong \ZZ$. By exactness at $\wt H_1(X)$,
$$
\ker\partial \cong \img\,\psi \cong \left(\wt H_1(U) \oplus \wt H_1(V)\right) / \ker\psi \cong \left(\wt H_1(U) \oplus \wt H_1(V)\right) / \img\,\phi \cong \ZZ,
$$
using our computation of $\img\,\phi$ from the previous slide (the subgroup generated by $(\alpha_U, -\alpha_V)$, whose quotient is $\ZZ$).

So we have a short exact sequence
$$
0 \to \underbrace{\ker\partial}_{\cong \ZZ} \injto \wt H_1(X) \surjto \underbrace{\img\,\partial}_{\cong \ZZ} \to 0.
$$

The right-hand term $\ZZ$ is free, so by the <Term k="tle71SplitExact">splitting lemma</Term> (coming up shortly) this sequence splits, giving $\wt H_1(X) \cong \ZZ \oplus \ZZ$ — matching the theorem.
