<SlideLayout title="Covering projections from group actions">

Here's a second way to manufacture covering projections, quite different from "wrapping" or "unrolling": start from a <Term k="groupAction">group action</Term> on a space.

Let $X$ be a topological space and $G$ a group acting on its points, so that each $g \in G$ gives a map $x \mapsto g \cdot x$. We require this map to be continuous for every $g$, and — crucially — that the <Term k="stabilizer">stabilizer</Term> of every point of $X$ is trivial (no nonidentity element of $G$ fixes any point).

Under these conditions we can form the <Term k="cp65QuotientByAction">quotient space</Term> $X/G$: fuse every <Term k="orbit">orbit</Term> of the action into a single point. There's a natural projection
$$
X \to X/G
$$
sending each point to the orbit it belongs to.

<Callout kind="definition" title="Regular projection">
Such a projection $X \to X/G$ is called a <Term k="cp65RegularProjection">regular</Term> projection. (An unfortunate choice of name, but a standard one.)
</Callout>
