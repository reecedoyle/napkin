The first half of the proposition — surjectivity — really is offensively easy once you know the lifting theorem.

<ProofReveal
  client:load
  id="cp65-lift-corr-proof-surjectivity"
  prompt="Prove: if E is path-connected, then Φ: π₁(B, b₀) → p⁻¹(b₀) is surjective."
  solution="Take any point e in the fiber p⁻¹(b₀). Since E is path-connected, there's a path η from e₀ to e in E. Then p ∘ η is a loop in B based at b₀ (because p(e₀) = p(e) = b₀), and by construction η is itself the lifting of p ∘ η starting at e₀. So Φ([p ∘ η]) = η(1) = e. Since e was an arbitrary point of the fiber, Φ hits every point of p⁻¹(b₀) — it's surjective."
