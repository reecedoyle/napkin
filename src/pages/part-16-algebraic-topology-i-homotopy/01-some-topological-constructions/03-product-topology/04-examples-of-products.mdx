A short list of products worth recognizing on sight:

<Callout kind="example" title="Familiar products">
- $\RR \times \RR$ is the Euclidean plane.
- $S^1 \times [0,1]$ is a cylinder.
- $S^1 \times S^1$ is a torus!
</Callout>

The first is immediate from the definition. The second is what you get sweeping a circle along a line segment. The third is worth pausing on: why should two circles multiplied together give a torus (a donut surface), rather than something else?
