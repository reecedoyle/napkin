<Callout kind="remark" title="Not embeddable in 3-space">
Unlike the torus, the <Term k="tc63KleinBottle">Klein bottle</Term> cannot be realized in ordinary 3-space without self-intersecting. You can tape the dashed edges together to get a cylinder as before, but then fusing the two solid-edge circles in *opposite* directions is impossible without passing the tube through itself. Pictures of the Klein bottle in 3D always tacitly allow this self-intersection.
</Callout>

Like the torus, it's a CW complex with one $0$-cell, two $1$-cells $e^1_a, e^1_b$, and a single $2$-cell — but this time attached via the word $abab^{-1}$ instead of $aba^{-1}b^{-1}$.
