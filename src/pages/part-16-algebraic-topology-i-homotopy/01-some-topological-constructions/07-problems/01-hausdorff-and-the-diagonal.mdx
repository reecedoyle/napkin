<Problem
  client:load
  id="tc63-prob-hausdorff-diagonal"
  difficulty="standard"
  prompt="Show that a space X is Hausdorff if and only if the diagonal {(x,x) : x in X} is closed in the product space X x X."
  solution="(⇒) Suppose X is Hausdorff and (x,y) is a point of X × X not on the diagonal, so x ≠ y. Pick disjoint open neighborhoods U of x and V of y. Then U × V is an open neighborhood of (x,y) in X × X, and it's disjoint from the diagonal: any (u,v) in U × V with u = v would put u in both U and V, contradicting disjointness. So the complement of the diagonal is open, i.e. the diagonal is closed. (⇐) Suppose the diagonal is closed, and let x ≠ y be points of X. Then (x,y) lies in the open complement of the diagonal, so some basic open rectangle U × V (with x in U, y in V) is entirely disjoint from the diagonal. That means no u in U equals any v in V, i.e. U and V are disjoint open neighborhoods of x and y. So X is Hausdorff."
