For R and R^n, tile by unit intervals or unit cubes with vertices at the integers (or integer lattice points)."
  solution="(a) Mobius strip: take one 0-cell v and one 1-cell forming a loop a based at v (this is the strip's core circle). Attach a single 2-cell whose boundary wraps around the loop a twice in the same direction (attaching word 'aa'), reflecting the half-twist in the strip — going once around the square's boundary drags you around the core circle twice before returning to your starting orientation. (b) R: take a 0-cell at every integer n, and a 1-cell joining each n to n+1. This infinite (but locally finite) 1-dimensional CW complex is exactly the real line. (c) R^n: take a 0-cell at every point of the integer lattice Z^n, a 1-cell joining each pair of adjacent lattice points, a 2-cell filling each unit square face, and so on up to n-cells filling each unit hypercube — the standard cubical CW structure on R^n."
