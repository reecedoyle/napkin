We're now ready for the star of the chapter.

<Callout kind="definition" title="Fundamental group">
The <Term k="fg64FundamentalGroup">fundamental group</Term> of $X$ with basepoint $x_0$, denoted $\pi_1(X, x_0)$, is the set of homotopy classes

$$
\left\{ [\gamma] \mid \gamma \text{ a loop at } x_0 \right\}
$$

equipped with $\ast$ as a group operation.
</Callout>

It might come as a surprise that this is actually a group — a set of equivalence classes of functions, with an operation that stitches two of them together. In particular: what plays the role of the inverse?
