<SlideLayout title="The figure eight">

Here's an example with a genuinely non-abelian fundamental group. Consider a figure eight $S^1 \vee S^1$ — two circles glued at a single point $x_0$ — with basepoint $x_0$.

<Callout kind="example" title="π₁ of the figure eight">
$$
\pi_1(S^1 \vee S^1, x_0) \cong \langle a, b \rangle,
$$
the <Term k="freeGroup">free group</Term> on two generators. One loop of the eight is $a$, the other is $b$; since the two loops don't "talk to each other" (there's no relation forcing, say, $ab = ba$), $\pi_1$ is generated freely by $a$ and $b$ (and their inverses $\overline a$, $\overline b$).
</Callout>
