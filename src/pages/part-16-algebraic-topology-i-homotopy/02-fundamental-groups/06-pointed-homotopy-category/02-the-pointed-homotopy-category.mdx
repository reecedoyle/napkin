<Callout kind="definition" title="Pointed homotopy category">
The <Term k="fg64PointedHomotopyCategory">pointed homotopy category</Term> $\mathbf{hTop}_\ast$ has:
- **objects**: pointed spaces $(X, x_0)$ — a space $X$ with a chosen basepoint,
- **morphisms**: homotopy classes of continuous based maps $(X, x_0) \to (Y, y_0)$.
</Callout>

In particular, two path-connected spaces are isomorphic in $\mathbf{hTop}_\ast$ exactly when they are <Term k="fg64HomotopyEquivalence">homotopy equivalent</Term> — everything from §5 was really a statement about isomorphism in this category.
