Reordering gives (dF1/dx + dF2/dy + dF3/dz)e1v^e2v^e3v."

<Problem
  client:load
  id="stk-prob-pullback-d-commute"
  difficulty="standard"
  prompt="Let phi: U -> U' be smooth. Prove that pullback and exterior derivative commute: phi*(d(alpha)) = d(phi*(alpha)) for any differential form alpha on U'."
  hint="This is a straightforward but somewhat lengthy computation. Write alpha in a basis, compute both sides, and compare. Use the chain rule for d(phi_i)."
  solution="Write alpha = sum f_I dw_I in a basis. Then phi*(alpha) = sum (f_I composed with phi)(D(phi_i1)^...^D(phi_ik)). Apply d to both sides. On the right, d(phi*(alpha)) differentiates f_I composed phi via the chain rule: d(f_I composed phi) = sum_j (df_I/dw_j composed phi) D(phi_j). On the left, phi*(d(alpha)) = phi*(sum df_I ^ dw_I). Expanding and applying the pullback definition gives the same expression: sum_I sum_j (df_I/dw_j composed phi) D(phi_j) ^ D(phi_i1) ^ ... ^ D(phi_ik)."
/>
