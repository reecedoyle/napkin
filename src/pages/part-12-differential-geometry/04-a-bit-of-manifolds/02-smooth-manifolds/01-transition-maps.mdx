<SlideLayout title="Transition maps">

Let $M$ be a topological $n$-manifold with atlas $\{U_i \xrightarrow{\phi_i} E_i\}$. When two charts overlap — $U_i \cap U_j \neq \varnothing$ — we need a way to pass between them.

<Callout kind="definition" title="Transition map">
For charts $\phi_i: U_i \to E_i$ and $\phi_j: U_j \to E_j$ with $U_i \cap U_j \neq \varnothing$, the <Term k="transitionMap">**transition map**</Term> is
$$\phi_{ij} \colon \phi_i(U_i \cap U_j) \xrightarrow{\phi_i^{-1}} U_i \cap U_j \xrightarrow{\phi_j} \phi_j(U_i \cap U_j).$$
It is a homeomorphism between open subsets of $\mathbb{R}^n$.
</Callout>

In plain language: the transition map tells you how to convert coordinates from chart $i$ to chart $j$ on their overlap. On $S^1$ covered by two arcs, the two transition maps are just coordinate changes on the two ends where the arcs overlap.

The key point: each $\phi_i$ and $\phi_j$ land in $\mathbb{R}^n$, so $\phi_{ij}$ is a map between open subsets of $\mathbb{R}^n$. This is where we know how to talk about smoothness.

</SlideLayout>
